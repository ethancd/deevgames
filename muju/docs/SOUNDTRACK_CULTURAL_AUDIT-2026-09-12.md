# Cultural influences and selected reference takes

The user requested a cultural-influence review and close variants with a bold cultural accent. Current bases were recovered from the listening page on 12 September 2026: Fire E has positive notes (C is another liked alternative); Plant 01A and Shadow 02A are favorites; Metal 10A was explicitly accepted; Water 13B is now a favorite. Lightning has no selected take yet. These are audition selections, not completed album masters.

**Rating limitation:** the scores below assess how specifically the written selected-take brief retains the original cultural idea. They are not reliable by-ear scores. A Google audio-understanding pass, authorized by the user, produced incompatible descriptions of Water 13B when processed with the group and alone. Its instrument and culture identifications were excluded. A score of 1 means a very general trace, 3 means a named musical voice with relevant phrasing, and 5 would require a strongly realized, verified arrangement. No tradition is reduced to a single instrument or certified authentic by these scores.

## Source trail

The [original pitch](SOUNDTRACK_PITCH-2026-09-11.md) contains the initial design decisions. Supporting sources:

- Fire: [Senzoku Gakuen koto guide](https://www.senzoku-online.jp/TMDL/e/01-koto.html), [Italian national instrument museum on koto](https://museostrumentimusicali.cultura.gov.it/strum/koto-cetra-2/).
- Lightning: [university-hosted Zanzibar recording discussion of taarab and kidumbak](https://www2.umbc.edu/eol/7/reed/index.html). Electrical scoring research is in the [separate brief](SOUNDTRACK_ELECTRICITY_RESEARCH-2026-09-12.md).
- Plant: [Ohio State Andean music guide](https://clas.osu.edu/andean-music-teacher-guide), [Smithsonian Folkways guide to Peru](https://folkways.si.edu/playlist/a-field-guide-to-peru).
- Metal: [Sissy Goodhouse on Lakota drumming](https://folkways.si.edu/lakota-drumming/american-indian/music/video/smithsonian), [Library of Congress on regional musical characteristics](https://www.loc.gov/collections/songs-of-america/articles-and-essays/musical-styles/american-indian-and-native-alaskan/). For the new flute accent, [NEA's Kevin Locke profile](https://www.arts.gov/honors/heritage/kevin-locke) documents his training in Lakota traditions and his work with Lakota/Dakota flute music. No existing song or performer is requested for imitation.
- Water: [Smithsonian Folkways Norwegian fiddle liner notes](https://folkways-media.si.edu/docs/folkways/artwork/FW04008.pdf).
- Shadow: [Türkiye culture ministry on bağlama](https://corum.ktb.gov.tr/TR-58740/muzik-kulturu-halk-muziği.html), [regional nine-unit rhythm discussion](https://isparta.ktb.gov.tr/TR-71007/isparta-turkuleri-hakkinda.html).
