## Caveats

- The adviser located these disagreements and judged none of them. Every claim above names judge 1, 2 or 4.
- The static number is a leaf evaluation of an end state. It is not the search's objective, which is a depth-2/3 minimax value from that same state, and it is not a claim about the turn's strength.
- `aiv2-hard`'s end state scoring above both candidates under the champion's own weights at 11 of 12 positions is a statement about those weights. It is not evidence that `aiv2-hard` chose better; the E1 baseline row says the champion beats it by +203 Elo.
- The 12 positions are the first-consequential turns of 14 losses out of the E1.1 baseline's 200 games, with `--max-turns 12` on the original analysis. They are not a sample of Muju positions and no frequency here generalises.
- Judge 1's +3/+6 snapshots are taken at the start of the seat's own turn and stop short of the mating sequences; five of the twelve games end `home-checkmate` after the +6 snapshot.
