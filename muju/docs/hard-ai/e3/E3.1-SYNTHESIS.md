## Conventions used below

- Scores are centi-crystals (cc) from the stated side's point of view; every feature is the symmetric difference `f(me) − f(them)`; `Material` is 100 cc per crystal of catalogue cost.
- "Judge" is one of E3-PLAN's four preregistered judges: 1 game outcome, 2 foreign engine (`aiv2-hard`), 3 authored judgment, 4 canonical fact. The champion at more work is a locator, never a judge (C1).
- "In-sample" means the evidence is measured on the development cases E3.2 would reproduce on: the 12 distinct E1.1 loss positions (lane 6) and the 16 exam loss roots that carry the same positions (lane 4, lane 7, lane 1's loss-root citations). "Out-of-sample" means fuzz, census, authored suites, or games on other openings.
- "Expected cost to play" (section 2) orders findings by magnitude in cc × how often the term fires on real positions × whether a judge gives a direction. It is an ordering argument, not an Elo estimate; no finding below has been priced by a contest row.

## 0. The answer in one paragraph

- Six things had to be settled before E3.2 could be chosen; five are settled and the sixth (the loss-dominant concept) is settled with two caveats that the recommendation carries.
- The evidence converges on one concept: the price the champion puts on a threatened own body — the safety block — is too high, and pays for itself with the rent and mining economy the losing turns gave up.
  - Judge 1, in-sample: at the 12 loss positions the safety group moves toward the played (losing) turn at 9 of the 10 positions where it moves at all (Σ −5,100 cc) while economy moves toward the adviser at 8 of 11 (Σ +3,613 cc); the material the played turn kept is gone three of the seat's own turns later at 12 of 12 (lane 6).
  - Judge 1, out-of-sample: zeroing the 19 safety weights beats the champion 14/0/2 at fixed work on development openings, +338 Elo [+168, +1200], 0.875 at both handicaps, with the exam exact count unchanged and the extra-search confound measured at +3.4% nodes (lane 5).
  - Judge 4: the `Hanging` feature's sign rests on multi-step, multi-attacker or purchase-enabled kills the rules cannot see in one step at 4 of 24 loss end states, and at the never-flip position `g4-s6_3_5-B` black t1 it carries 91% of the disagreement while the rules find no one-step kill for either side at either end state (lane 6).
  - Judge 3 and code: one vulnerable unit is charged through Exposure, Hanging/HangingBuy, ApproachRetreat/Strand and the opponent's KillAvailable at once, mean 313 cc against a 391 cc prior, up to 130% of the unit; DESIGN §5.12.1 row 17's own rationale calls Exposure "coarse hanging; refined at stage 2" and stage 2 adds rather than replaces (lane 1).
- Recommendation: E3.2's first row prices that concept with the arm that already exists, `hard@ablate:eval-no-safety` (lane 5's `weights` factor, the 19 safety weights at 0, material untouched, `hard@desktop` byte-identical), as a wall:3000 screening row on `e1-val2.jsonl` rows 0–15, 32 pairs, seed 20260931, with the preregistered prediction score > 0.5. Its development case is `g4-s6_3_5-B-white` black turn 1, where the block carries −335 cc of a −275 cc gap and the same two end states score +60 cc for the adviser with the block off.
- Three preconditions come before that row's non-regression gates mean anything: `suites/run.ts` runs every engine-searched suite with the version-0 placeholder weights (lane 7), so the tactics suite cannot see a weights arm until the one-line fix lands and A6's baselines are re-measured; the exam's 25,000-unit budget flips margin cases; and E3-PLAN's screening list names `e1-val.jsonl` rows 16–31, which E2 row #2 already consumed as its "second and last use".
- Five engine defects are bugs by a canonical fact or by the engine's own specification and must be fixed regardless of the row's outcome; each still needs its own switchable arm and its own row before it can touch the champion (section 5).

## 1. The six things E3.1 had to settle

### 1.1 Which features double count — SETTLED (judge 4 for every identity; judge 3 where DESIGN prices one fact in two rows)

EPIC-PLAN §4's specific question first.

- "Crystal bank, projected mining and finite reserves must not count the same future resources three times": they do not, on lane 3's reading (`E3.1-ECONOMY-AUDIT.md` ledger 4, judge 4).
  - Bank crystals are read by `BankLiquid`/`BankExcess`/`BankConvertible`.
  - Crystals under a miner are read by `PstMine` and `EconDelta`.
  - Crystals in the ground inside the spawn rectangle are read by `SpawnReserve`, restricted to empty squares by `core/spawn.ts:99`, so a square is never in both sets.
  - What is wrong is the coefficient, not the count: a crystal mined on own-turn t is worth +0.52·γᵗ·100 cc for t ≤ 6 and −0.20·γᵗ·100 cc for 7 ≤ t ≤ 12, because `EconDelta` subtracts `pstSum` at weight 80 while `PstMine` adds it at 60 (L3-F2, L1-F10; measured +420 cc over crystals 1–6 and −40 cc over crystals 7–12 of a mine-1 miner, `ledger.json` sweep `reserve-sweep-mine1`).

The double counts, each confirmed by reading the code, with its measurement.

- Identical predicates.
  - `SpawnZero` and `Inv1SpawnZero`: `tables/geometry.ts:102` and `eval/invariants.ts:290` are character-for-character the same test, both at −800, so the condition costs −1,600 cc (L2-F1, L3-F6, L4-F2). Co-fire on 89 of 89 positions where either is non-zero across 2,151 positions; 0 of 16 loss roots.
  - `RunwayCliff` and `Insolvency` at turn 1: `turnsToInsolvency === 1` is exactly `bank + income[0] < upkeep[0]`, so one condition costs −600 + 5×(−150) = −1,350 cc (L3-F9; r = 0.962 on 35 exam-dev positions; 0 of 16 loss roots).
  - `HomeInvaded`'s predicate is `home.occupied` verbatim, which sets invariant 10's first limb, and `CornerInfiltration`'s first clause is the same predicate from the other side: one unit on the enemy corner is priced 4,000 + 400 + 300 = 4,700 cc on 126 of the 128 positions where `HomeInvaded` fires (L2-F4, L4-F3 as corrected by review); 0 of 16 loss roots, 44 of 56 home-mate.
- The same quantity, twice.
  - Rent: `rentCrystals` (`features.ts:158-166`) and `upkeepDue` (`income.ts:94-102`) are the same sum; `economy.ts:175` freezes it and `economy.ts:221` subtracts it on all six horizon turns inside `stream`, which `EconDelta` carries at w = 80. One crystal per turn of upkeep costs −422 (Rent) −305 (EconDelta's leg) = −727 cc, 1.72× the `RENT_PV` DESIGN §5.12 (DESIGN.md:1348, "charged **once**, as `Rent`") states (L1-F1, L3-F1; measured −742 cc at loss root `loss-g2-s5_0_2-A-white-t4`, `water_1 → water_2`).
  - `DepletionWaste ≡ 6·Σmine − Σ income_t` over the same six-turn DP that produces `stream`, exact in 1,659 of 2,000 fuzz side-positions (L1-F5); +30 cc per crystal of realised income and −180 cc per point of mine rate, undiscounted, on top of `EconDelta`'s discounted copy.
  - `blocking ≤ 1` is priced by `AnchorFragility` (+2 fragility, −240 cc), `BlockingDeficit` (−150/−300) and `Inv6FragileAnchor` (−120): up to −660 cc, and 1,094 of 2,000 side-positions sit at `blocking ≤ 1` (L1-F4).
- Nested predicates on one fact.
  - `HomeThreat` (−400) ⊃ invariant 10's second limb (−400), inside `HomeCountdown`'s non-zero set (−180/−360): 400 + 400 + 180 or 360 cc on 509 positions across the corpora where the two fire together with identical sign and magnitude (L2-F4).
  - `BankLiquid` (90 cc per crystal below 8) and `BankConvertible` (20) read the same crystal: 110 cc per banked crystal below 8, 45 cc above, against exactly 100 cc for the same crystal turned into a body (L3-F5, measured step by step in `ledger.json` sweep `bank-sweep`).

## 3. The E3.2 recommendation

### 3.1 The one concept

- The price of a threatened own body: what the evaluator charges for a unit the opponent could kill next turn, before the opponent has spent an action.
- The block that carries it is `eval-groups.ts`'s safety group: Exposure, Hanging, HangingBuy, ApproachRetreat, ApproachStrand, StrandPunish, KillAvailable, CleaveExposure, AnchorFragility, BlockingDeficit and the nine safety invariants (3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 12, 17, 19, 20).
