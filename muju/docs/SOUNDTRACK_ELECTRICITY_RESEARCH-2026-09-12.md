# Electricity: finding the physical sensation

Research for Umeme, 12 September 2026. This is a creative synthesis of documented scoring decisions and listening references, not a claim that the reference recordings were directly auditioned in this session.

The most useful working image is stored energy becoming sudden movement. Our major-key plucks already describe nimbleness. A stronger elemental identity could come from a held charge, a tiny interruption, and a decisive musical arrival. This is a compositional hypothesis, not a universal musical code for electricity.

## Documented examples and listening references

- **Raiden Shogun, Genshin Impact — stillness and action.** In a published first-person lecture, HOYO-MiX producer Yuan Dimeng explains that the character's duality motivated a combination of orchestra, Japanese elements, trap, and drum and bass, switching between motion/stillness and fast/slow. The documented intention concerns character portrayal; applying that contrast to Umeme's electrical charge is our inference. [Lecture transcript published by Game Grape](https://www.taptap.cn/moment/386236913759029674), [official character demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mvrW4aKwAXw).
- **Electro, The Amazing Spider-Man 2 — the body shivering.** Director Marc Webb described Zimmer finding the theme's electrical pulse in the shivering cadence of a seventeenth-century aria. This is a particularly useful alternative to simply asking for electronic instruments. A separate direct interview describes warm clarinet, a repeating guitar idea, and heavier guitar texture as Electro becomes dangerous. [Webb's account](https://www.slashfilm.com/530740/marc-webb-talks-plethora-villains-amazing-spider-man-2/), [direct score-making interview](https://www.vanityfair.com/video/watch/inside-hans-zimmers-amazing-spider-man-2-score).
- **Zinogre, Monster Hunter — electrical power embodied in a creature.** Use the theme as a reference for electricity with physical confidence. Capcom documents a rock concert arrangement of Tadayoshi Makino's theme; the game's theme combines electric-guitar identity with Japanese instrumental color, also described in the series music reference. [Official artist recording](https://open.spotify.com/track/6fqpahPjK9SXAY2oAKrH3w), [Capcom concert account](https://news.capcomusa.com/lets/browse/capcom-recap-06-09-2017-06-22-2017), [secondary theme documentation](https://monsterhunterwiki.org/wiki/Zinogre_%28Music%29).
- **Ramuh, FFXIV — thunder as a deity.** The official development account identifies a new vocalist and a stanza in the fictional Old High Sylphic language for “Thunder Rolls.” Here the element has a voice and a religious/character context. Our lesson is that a thunder theme can invoke presence and ritual rather than literal sparks. [Official development blog](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/blog/000788.html), [recording](https://open.spotify.com/track/1Qw2cJY1WmtHXZggiQR9Lw).
- **Thunder Plains, FFX — an environmental comparison.** Include this alongside the boss/character references to test how much lighter motion belongs in our cue. Square Enix also offers a piano arrangement, a useful reminder that this setting need not depend on electronic instrumentation. [Official original soundtrack audio](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dkugU4qqHKo), [official piano collection](https://www.jp.square-enix.com/music/en/lineup/item/SQEX-10028.html).

## Three proposed Umeme directions

| Direction | Physical image | Proposed musical behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Held charge | Hair lifting before a strike; relaxed but intensely alert | A quiet sustained tone gradually acquires an edge. The hook waits through a short gap, then lands with a clean plucked attack and a strong bass answer. |
| Chain lightning | A spark jumps between targets | One recognizable melodic cell passes between acoustic pluck and electric lead. Unequal phrase lengths and brief silences make the leaps deliberate. |
| Joyful current | Confident movement powered from within | A warm, settled groove with a singing electric bass. Short surges of busier rhythm return quickly to the relaxed pulse. |

My recommendation is **Held charge with Chain lightning's answering hook**. Keep major-key clarity, comfortable treble, and a memorable tune. Use local harmonic suspense and phrase timing to create anticipation; a minor-key rewrite is unnecessary. Preserve the Swahili-coast udi/kanuni connection as a distinctive voice within the electrical arrangement.

For repeated background listening, make electrical events part of the musical phrases rather than constant crackles, fast arpeggios, or large cinematic drops. Low rolling thunder would overlap with Metal's weight; continuous legato would overlap with Water. Umeme's distinguishing behavior can be readiness followed by a precise leap.
