# Phone UI and incoming moves

- Gameplay uses the dynamic viewport height in portrait and phone landscape. The board takes the remaining space after the compact controls, including timed Phasing games.
- Post-game analysis uses that same bounded viewport. The old auto-height Phasing layout left the review grid without a definite board height, collapsing it to a sliver. Analysis controls now span the footer, with compact phone spacing and landscape rows that account for the hidden decision panel. Empty summoning rows do not consume analysis space.
- Clocks stay visible and continue reflecting authoritative server time. Room links, reconnect credentials, and clock explanations are available through **Room**. Phasing and arrival details expand above the footer.
- Tap a pending summon to inspect its stats and full-turn attack reach on the current board. The preview assumes a supported arrival; it does not make the pending piece an occupant or an actionable unit. An occupied square retains its normal unit tap and offers a separate small summon control. Summons can also be inspected from the phasing list and during an opponent's turn.
- New remote actions play in order at the Instant replay speed: Fast is 300 ms per action and Slow is 1,000 ms. Manual Step mode uses Slow for incoming moves. Multi-action movement is split into speed-sized hops, followed by attacks and subsequent preparation/arrival events.
- Incoming updates queue while earlier actions are playing. Initial loads do not replay old history; the local player's acknowledgements remain immediate. Gameplay is disabled until playback catches up. Hidden tabs pause presentation, and clock samples do not add playback frames. Missing history, undo, or incompatible reconstructed results fall back to the server snapshot.

Regression coverage lives in `e2e/phone-playback.spec.ts`, the existing mobile/online/replay suites, and `tests/hooks/incoming-playback.test.tsx` and `tests/hooks/enemy-reach.test.tsx`. `e2e/analysis.spec.ts` also covers online Phasing timeout results with pending summons, board dimensions, reach inspection, reloads, review navigation, and private variations across small phones, landscape, and desktop. The online result remains unchanged after exploration.
