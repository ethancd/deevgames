PIP: Helmet fitted. I am ready for the safety round.

CLICK: There is no round. The helmet is not defense. Nothing here is timed.

PIP: Then I will answer slowly. Helmet on.

CLICK: From the drawer. Proposal: the helmet counts as defense. Overruled. The card says one.

COACH: Hello. Somebody wants our Hi.

COACH: The Sjor's speed is one. One square, one action.

COACH: The Sjor's attack is two. Water beats Fire, so add one. That makes three.

COACH: Three damage against defense one. A hit removes the Hi.

PIP: Then the Hi steps back one square, to D three.

COACH: The Sjor cannot stand on our Hi. It must stop next door, on D four.

COACH: That trip is five squares. Five squares is five actions.

COACH: Then the hit. Six.

CLICK: Six is their whole turn. It fits.

CLICK: Count their trip. Then count their hit.

CLICK: Their whole turn: six actions.

CLICK: Hitting the Hi on D three would take all six.

COACH: Now the Hi stands on D two. Can this Sjor reach it and hit it?

COACH: Say it, point at a podium, or take the time and say nothing.

COACH: To hit D two, the Sjor must stand on D three. That trip is six squares.

COACH: The Sjor arrived beside it, with no action left to attack. So: no.

CLICK: The hit would be a seventh action. There is no seventh action.

CLICK: Name for the file: the Missing Seventh.

COACH: Our Hi has speed two. D four to D two was one trip, one action.

CLICK: The counting did it. The helmet has filed a complaint.

CLICK: The Sjor is standing on D three now. Next turn it is one action away.

CLICK: Six squares. Speed two. Three actions, then the hit. Four.

PIP: So D two was never the safe part. The Sjor's speed was.

COACH: One to ask whoever plays with you. Which retreat would you pick, and why?

CLICK: Count their trip. Then count their hit. The buzzer stays in the drawer.

COACH: Today was plenty. Next time, a clock that wakes for two reasons.
