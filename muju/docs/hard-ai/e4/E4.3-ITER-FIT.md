# E4.3 candidate B — the iteration-cost estimator (`searchFix.iterFit`)

Lane 5 of the E4 plan, on `claude/hard-ai-e4-lane5` off the plan commit
`437ee40f`. Opened 2026-09-18 00:02:14Z; every stamp below is from `date -u`.

The question E2 left: the estimator that decides whether the next iteration is
funded is a single previous-iteration cost ratio clamped at 6, the measured
ratios have median 9.05 per depth, the gate arms changed nothing because the
floor never binds, and the champion completes depth 2-3 at a 200,000-unit rung
where the E2 flips need 283,000-800,000 (`E2-PLAN.md` "iteration cost ratio",
`E2-LANE1-DEEP-GATE.md` §E, `E2-BASELINE-LOSSES.md`).

## 1. The rule

Active only when `config.searchFix.iterFit === true`. Absent — every profile,
the champion, every other arm — nothing below runs and the search is the search
it was (§4).

Notation. At the moment depths 1..d of THIS search have completed and depth
d+1 is being considered: `limit` is the work rung (`meter.limit`), `used` the
work spent (`meter.used`), `c_d` what the last completed depth cost and
`c_{d-1}` what the one before it cost — aspiration re-searches included in
both. A TRUNCATED iteration is not a sample of what a depth costs and is never
recorded, which is the existing rule, unchanged.

1. **The step ratio** `r` (`search/time.ts:387 iterStepRatio`):
   - `r = (c_d / c_{d-1}) × 0.25` when this search has two completed depths to
     divide — THIS turn's own measurement, one step stale;
   - otherwise the previous WALL-funded search's measurement of THIS step, from
     the engine's per-step prior (`search/time.ts:346 IterCostPrior`, an
     α = 1/4 EWMA per step);
   - otherwise 12.
   - Clamped to `[2, 40]`.
2. **The prediction** `ĉ = c_d × r` (`search/time.ts:416 iterFitDecision`).
3. **Fund** the iteration when `ĉ ≤ (limit − used) × 1.25`. It then runs,
   completes, publishes and is deepened past exactly as any iteration does.
4. **Otherwise spend the remainder**, while `limit − used ≥ limit / 8`: run
   depth d+1 anyway, as a partial, with no extra budget, and stop after it. It
   may replace the published move only when all three of
   (a) the candidate loop was ended by the WORK METER and not by the deadline
       watchdog (`search/pvs.ts:772`), so the move is a function of the rung
       and not of the clock (DESIGN §5.11.6: the watchdog "can only truncate …
       never alter a completed depth");
   (b) the candidate carrying the currently published move was searched to the
       end at this depth with `s.truncated` still false — the principal
       variation COMPLETED at the new depth (`search/pvs.ts:720`);
   (c) another candidate, also searched to the end and untruncated, scored
       strictly higher than it (`search/pvs.ts:881 partialPublishes`).
   The comparison that publishes is then between two fully searched candidates
   at the same depth, which is the only comparison a partial iteration can
   honestly make. `result.depth` still reports the last COMPLETED depth; only
   the move and its score move (`search/pvs.ts:969`).
5. **Otherwise stop** and return the last completed depth, as today.

Each step's measured ratio is folded into the prior as the depth completes
(`search/pvs.ts:999`).

### Why these numbers, and what each one's sample is

- `0.25` (`ITER_STEP_DECAY`, `time.ts:294`). E2 lane 1 split its 126
  consecutive-completed-iteration pairs by step: depth 1 → 2 has median 12.32
  (n = 19, min 3.66, max 39.17), depth 2 → 3 median 3.02 (n = 17, min 0.61, max
  10.74). 12.32 × 0.25 = 3.08 predicts 3.02. The ratio is not one distribution
  but one per step, which is exactly what a single previous ratio cannot see.
- `12` (`ITER_RATIO_COLD`, `time.ts:284`). E2's depth 1 → 2 median, rounded
  down; used only when this turn has one completed depth and the prior is empty.
- `[2, 40]` (`time.ts:298`, `:303`). The floor stops the third extrapolation:
  at the 3 → 4 step, 3.02 × 0.25 = 0.755 would predict a deeper iteration
  CHEAPER than its predecessor, which no measured pair supports. The ceiling is
  E2's largest measured ratio (39.17) rounded up; it is not the binding clamp
  the E2 estimator's 6 was, it only stops a near-zero predecessor producing an
  unbounded prediction.
- `1.25` (`ITER_FIT_SLACK`, `time.ts:318`) — **the stated margin**. The
  prediction may exceed the remaining budget by 25% before the iteration is
  refused. It points that way because the estimator's residual error is
  one-sided (a model built from two steps and floored over-predicts more often
  than it under-predicts) and because the downside is now bounded: an iteration
  that is funded and then does not fit is no longer work thrown away, since
  rule 4 lets a non-completing iteration publish when its principal variation
  completed. A margin BELOW 1 reproduces `deep-gate`'s refusals, which is the
  failure this arm exists to fix.
- `1/8` (`ITER_REMAINDER_MIN_SHARE`, `time.ts:328`). Below an eighth of the
  rung the remainder cannot complete one root candidate's subtree at the new
  depth, so running it buys nothing and only pushes the turn toward the
  deadline (P6: a turn over 20 s withholds the timing columns).

The remainder is `limit − used` in BOTH modes, because the rung is the budget
in both. The wall deadline is not predicted: A11's watchdog is a separate
backstop and the search cannot price a millisecond in work units.

### What it is not

- Not a wider root re-search. The root already searches every candidate the
  generator returned, so "wider" means a larger `gen.K` — generation, which is
  lane 4's file and a different factor. Considered and rejected on that ground.
- Not a change to what a COMPLETED depth means, and it never publishes a score
  that was not searched.
- Not `deep-gate` with a different clamp. E2 showed the clamp is inert
  (lowering the floor from 2 to 1.3 changed work, depth and rung by zero units
  on 20 positions); the estimator is what was wrong.

### The prior, and the determinism contract

The per-step prior is the one thing carried across turns, and it is carried the
way `config.profile` is: **`engine.ts:429` clears it at the start of every
FIXED-work search**. A fixed-work search therefore still carries nothing from
one search into the next, which is DESIGN §7.4's contract and `hard:determinism`'s
whole premise; the prior is a wall-mode quantity that a wall-funded turn may
read from the previous wall-funded turn of the same engine. In practice it
decides one thing: whether depth 2 starts, where this turn has completed exactly
one iteration and has no step of its own to divide.

Per position, completed iterations are **equal on 18 of 20, up on 1, down on

rule:

- `e1-g3-s105_0_12-A-white:t6` — champion rung 100,000, arm rung 200,000; 2

- `e1-g4-s430_0_36-A-white:t6` — champion rung 200,000, arm rung 100,000; 3
  iterations against 2, 90 root replies against 68.

The rung is chosen from `profile.unitsPerMs`, which the warm-ups measure, and a
search that spends more of its rung amortises the fixed per-search cost over
more work and so measures a slightly higher throughput. The arm therefore moves
the rung as well as the schedule, in both directions, on 3 of 20 positions.
**On the 17 positions where both arms landed on the same rung**, completed
iterations are identical (2.47 each), mean work is 148,488 against 164,856
(+11.0%) and mean root replies 75.8 against 78.5 (+3.6%). No position at an
equal rung loses an iteration.

**What this says, plainly.** At the rungs this box reaches (100,000 and
200,000; E2's probe reached 200,000 throughout, on a less loaded machine) the
estimator does NOT buy completed iterations — the next depth genuinely does not
fit, and the honest prediction says so where the 0.45 rule says so. What it
buys is the 16.5 points of the rung the champion leaves unspent (83.5% →
100.1%), 3.6% more searched root replies at an equal rung, and a different
principal turn on 4 of 20 positions, three of them through the
principal-variation publication rule. Whether those turns are BETTER is not a
question this probe can answer — the equal-time ladder row is (E4 plan,
usefulness rule 4), and the champion at more work is a locator, never a judge
(E3's C1).

Abort share is 0 in both arms, so nothing here is withheld by the P6 flag and
no turn came near the 20 s threshold.
