# E3.1 — feature contributions, scale and cost (lane 4)

Instrument: `npm run hard:eval-audit`, `lab/hard-ai/audit/eval-audit.ts` with
its loader `lab/hard-ai/audit/eval-corpus.ts`. Groups come from
`lab/hard-ai/audit/eval-groups.ts` (coordinator-owned; imported, never edited).
Tests: `tests/lab/eval-audit.test.ts`, 10 cases, green.

Everything below is measured with `DEFAULT_WEIGHTS` (`default-v1`, version 1,
`weightsHash` `15b9b4e2`) through `Evaluator.full(p, root, sc, 0, outFeatures)`.
No engine is constructed; the evaluator is built directly and
`DEFAULT_WEIGHTS.version !== 0` is asserted before any measurement
(`eval-audit.ts newCtx`), which is E0's I2 lesson in the form E3-PLAN.md's rules
section states it.

Judges used in this document:

- Judge 4 (canonical fact) for every arithmetic identity and every symmetry
  check: the claim is decided by the engine's own output plus the geometry the
  rules define, not by any search.
- No claim here rests on the adviser or on the champion at more work.

## Symmetry

Two checks, run per position, both finer than the M12 gate's.

- SIDE SWAP. `f_i(p, side)` against `−f_i(p, 1 − side)`, no rotation. Nothing is
  moved, so relocation's argmax tie-break cannot excuse a failure.
  - 0 violations on all 2,151 positions, all 58 features.
  - `full(p, side) === −full(p, 1 − side)` on all 2,151 positions.
- ROT180. `mirror180(state)` (`lab/hard-ai/positions/corpus.ts`) rotates the
  board 180° and swaps the seats, so the player who was `side` is `1 − side` in
  the mirror; per feature `f_i(mirror, 1 − side)` must EQUAL `f_i(p, side)`.
  - No `PackedState` transform is exported to do this. `src/ai/hard/book/probe.ts`
    has `mirrorInto` — the packed rotation `canonicalKey` uses — but it is
    module-private and writes into one shared buffer; only
    `canonicalKey(p) -> {lo, hi, negated}` is exported, and a key cannot be
    evaluated. The audit therefore rotates the canonical `GameState` and
    re-packs, and the check written above is `negated: true` spelled out.
  - Violations occur in exactly three features and nowhere else, on every
    corpus: `EconDelta` (23), `DepletionWaste` (24), `RelocationDebt` (27).
    All three are `economy` group, all three stage 2.
  - No violation in any of the other 55 features on any of the 2,151 positions,
    including the 613 handicap-3 `fuzz-1000` positions and the four corpora
    (`tactics`, `home-mate`, `invariants`, `exam-dev`) the M12 bench gate never
    reads.

`Δ score` is `Σ_i w[i] · (f_i(mirror, 1 − side) − f_i(p, side))` over the
violating features: how many centi-crystals the two spellings of the same
position disagree by.

- The cause is already on record: `tables/economy.ts bestRelocationTarget`
  breaks an exact argmax tie by "lowest square index" and `s ↦ 99 − s` reverses
  that order, so no deterministic total order on squares survives the rotation
  (`lab/hard-ai/bench/run.ts` header, DEVIATIONS under M12).
- What is new here is the size and the reach. On `openings` the mean
  disagreement is 241 cc against a mean \|score\| of 754 cc — 32% of the typical
  score magnitude, on 24% of the corpus. On `home-mate` it is a flat 480 cc on
  8 of 56 positions. This is not a rounding-level artefact.

## Features that never fire

Three features are zero on all 2,151 positions of all seven corpora:

| # | feature | group | stage | w | why |
| ---: | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 11 | Infiltration | home | 1 | +90 | structurally identically zero — see below |
| 52 | Inv15UnknownAsSafe | space | 2 | 0 | DESIGN §5.13 gives it no number ("structural: any UNKNOWN prover verdict is scored as the bad case"); `weights.ts` sets `w = 0` deliberately |
| 55 | Inv18WastedEndPlace | space | 2 | 0 | DESIGN §5.13 calls it a "protocol rule only … not a feature (bit always 0)"; `weights.ts` sets `w = 0` deliberately |

Two more features fire but are weighted 0 by DESIGN §5.12.1 and so contribute
nothing: `Corridor` (20, fires on 28.0% of `fuzz-1000`) and `TierClimb` (21,
fires on 47.3%).

`Infiltration` is the one that is not by design. It is identically zero on every
position, for a geometric reason, not because the corpora missed the case:

- `eval/features.ts:284` — `out[F.Infiltration] = gMe.infiltrationAnchors − gThem.infiltrationAnchors`.
- `tables/geometry.ts:124-137` — for `side`, `infiltrationAnchors` counts pairs
  (own unit on square `s`, enemy unit on square `a`) with `bbHas(RECT[enemy][a], s)`.
- `core/tables.ts:95-119` — `RECT[side][anchor]` is the axis-aligned box between
  that side's start corner and the anchor; `board.ts:176-178`  puts White's corner
  at (0,0) and Black's at (9,9); `spawning.ts:8-29` builds the inclusive box.
- So for White: `s ∈ RECT[BLACK][a]` ⟺ `s.x ≥ a.x and s.y ≥ a.y`. For Black:
  `s ∈ RECT[WHITE][a]` ⟺ `s.x ≤ a.x and s.y ≤ a.y`. Relabelling Black's pair
  `(s, a)` as `(a, s)` turns the second condition into the first: both sides
  count the SAME set of (white unit, black unit) ordered pairs, so the two
  counts are equal on every position and the symmetric difference is 0.
- Measured, not only argued: over 1,876 positions (`fuzz-1000` + `openings` +
  `tactics`) the raw per-side counts reach 15 and are equal on 1,876 of 1,876 —
  1,305 both zero, 571 equal and nonzero, 0 unequal.
- A weight of +90 on a term that can never be anything but 0 is 90 cc of
  DESIGN §5.12.1's intent that the engine has never spent. The fix belongs to
  E3.2 and is not attempted here; `src/ai/hard/**` is untouched.

## Double counting

Pearson correlation of `c_i = w[i] · f_i` across positions, both features
nonzero in at least `max(5, 2% of n)` positions. The full top-20 per corpus is
in each `summary.json` under `correlatedPairs`; a pair with a low
`bothNonzero` is a mean-driven artefact of disjoint supports, not evidence, and
is excluded from the reading below.

Three pairs are confirmed duplicates by reading the code, not only by the
correlation:

- `SpawnZero` (9, w = −800) and `Inv1SpawnZero` (38, w = −800) are the SAME
  predicate, written twice.
  - `tables/geometry.ts:102` — `out.zeroCliff = info.area === 0 && p.bank[side] >= 3 ? 1 : 0`.
  - `eval/invariants.ts:290` — `if (geom.area === 0 && p.bank[side] >= 3) bits |= bit(1)`.
  - Measured `r = +1.000` on `fuzz-1000` (42 positions both nonzero), `exam-dev`
    (15) and `tactics` (8); on `fuzz-1000` both fire on exactly 4.2% of
    positions with identical mean \|w·f\| of 33.6 cc.
  - A side with no spawn area and three crystals in hand is therefore charged
    1,600 cc, not the 800 DESIGN §5.12.1 prints and the 800 DESIGN §5.13 prints.
- `HomeInvaded` (4, w = −4000) and `CornerInfiltration` (37, w = +300) score the
  same event from the two ends.
  - `eval/features.ts:167-171` — `homeInvaded(p, side)` is `[an enemy unit stands
    on side's own corner]`.
  - `eval/features.ts:384-395` — `cornerInfiltration(p, side)` is `[own unit on
    the ENEMY corner]` or `[both enemy corner neighbours held by me]`.
  - The first clause of the second is the negation of the first, so an own unit
    standing on the enemy corner pays out `+4000` through `HomeInvaded` and a
    further `+300` through `CornerInfiltration`: 4,300 cc for one unit on one
    square.
  - Measured `r = +1.000` on `fuzz-1000` (42 both nonzero), `exam-dev` (34),
    `tactics` (8); both fire on exactly the same 4.2% of `fuzz-1000`.
- `CornerSeal` (12, w = −60) and `Inv2CornerSeal` (39, w = −300) read the same
  counter.
  - `eval/features.ts:285` — `out[F.CornerSeal] = gMe.cornerNeighboursHeld − gThem.cornerNeighboursHeld`.
  - `eval/invariants.ts:293` — `if (geom.cornerNeighboursHeld === 2) bits |= bit(2)`.
  - The invariant is the threshold-2 case of the linear feature, so sealing both
    neighbours costs `2 × 60 + 300 = 420` cc, not 300. `Inv2CornerSeal` fires on
    0.8% of `fuzz-1000` against `CornerSeal`'s 27.4%, so the overlap is narrow
    but real.

## Cost

Per-position cost, cold tables: one timed pass per variant over the whole
corpus in list order so the evaluator's one-position table cache
(`NodeTables.keyLo`/`keyHi`) misses on every call, 5 repetitions, per-position
median, then mean and 95th percentile across positions. Stage 1 and stage 2 are
differences of the measured cumulative passes. The BFS distance cache inside
`NodeTables` is NOT invalidated between positions — the same regime
`bench/run.ts --eval`'s throughput row uses. M2 Max, load from this lane only.

| corpus | stage 0 µs | stage 1 µs | stage 2 µs | full µs | full p95 µs | stage 2 share of full |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| fuzz-1000 | 0.60 | 21.08 | 87.88 | 109.56 | 204.38 | 80.2% |
| openings | 0.54 | 12.64 | 26.26 | 39.44 | 46.38 | 66.6% |
| economy | 1.34 | 16.39 | 23.62 | 41.35 | 51.42 | 57.1% |
| tactics | 0.95 | 13.35 | 21.98 | 36.28 | 47.25 | 60.6% |
| home-mate | 1.12 | 14.27 | 16.75 | 32.13 | 37.50 | 52.1% |
| invariants | 1.19 | 21.34 | 28.38 | 50.91 | 68.29 | 55.7% |
| exam-dev | 0.74 | 11.77 | 27.16 | 39.67 | 75.04 | 68.5% |

- Stage 0 is 0.5–1.3 µs and never more than 3.5% of a full call: the five
  incremental features cost nothing.
- Stage 2 is 52–80% of a full call on every corpus. DESIGN §5.12.4's lazy exit
  (`boundStage2`) is the only thing standing between the search and that cost,
  which is why the M12 lazy-window gate matters more than its size suggests.
- The spread across corpora is 3.4× on stage 2 alone (`fuzz-1000` 87.9 µs
  against `home-mate` 16.8 µs) and the `fuzz-1000` p95 of a full call is 204 µs
  against a 110 µs mean. A crowded position costs several times what a sparse
  one does; any per-node evaluation budget derived from a mean will be wrong on
  the positions that matter.

## Identities checked on every position

All clean on all 2,151 positions; a failure in any of these would invalidate
every table above.

- `Σ_i w[i] · f_i === full()` — 0 mismatches.
- `stage0() + stage1() + stage2() === full()` — 0 mismatches.
- The five `eval-groups.ts` group sums add up to `full()` — 0 mismatches.
- The three `STAGE_OF` stage sums add up to `full()` — 0 mismatches.
- Material residual `w[Material] · f[Material] − stage0`'s own material block —
  0 nonzero. `Evaluator.stage0` scores feature 0 from the 18 `w.material`
  parameters while `extract` writes the weight-free catalogue prior; with
  `DEFAULT_WEIGHTS` the two agree exactly, which the audit measures rather than
  assumes.

One documentation contradiction found while wiring this up, reported and not
edited (`src/ai/hard/**` is untouched):

- `eval/features.ts:28-31` says `full()` "overwrites `out[F.Material]` with the
  weighted value".
- `eval/evaluate.ts:23-24` says "`full`'s `outFeatures[Material]` carries
  `extract`'s weight-free prior version for Texel's reporting".
- `eval/evaluate.ts:167` does the latter: `outFeatures.set(this.f)` and nothing
  else. The measured material residual of 0 shows the difference is invisible
  under `DEFAULT_WEIGHTS`; it would not be under a tuned `material` block, which
  is E3.3's problem. The proposal is in `amendments/lane4.md`.

## Caveats

- Every number is static evaluation. No search ran, so nothing here says what
  the champion PLAYS; it says what it would score a position at.
- The corpora are not a sample of anything. `openings` is 797 turn-1 census
  nodes, `economy`/`tactics`/`home-mate`/`invariants` are authored suites built
  to exercise particular features, and `fuzz-1000` is the only broad one.
  Shares should be compared within a corpus, never pooled across them.
- Timings are one box under this lane's load only, with the BFS distance cache
  warm across positions. They are a profile, not a budget.
- The correlation table is Pearson on contributions. A high `r` with a small
  `bothNonzero` means disjoint supports and a mean-driven coefficient, not a
  shared term; only the three pairs confirmed by reading the source are claimed
  as double counts.
- 16 loss roots is a small sample and the bucket comparison above is
  descriptive.
