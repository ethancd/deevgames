## Adversarial assessment and limits

The shorter roster is easier to enumerate, but cutting the crown did not solve stalling in these bots. Mobility helps Tanka reach a role without strengthening its corner DEF; it still needs coordinated protection and can still fall to two Karanlık. Eliminating Fire IV's four-kill sweep and the old premium invaders removes finishing power as well as defensive walls. The unchanged roaming–home deadlock is evidence against claiming a general endgame cure.

Single-piece, open-board static witnesses do not establish mixed-army balance or how often a role occurs. Scripted policies use fixed priorities and bounded home-defense search; they are not optimal opponents or human playtests. E9 deliberately retains its old uniform board and elimination-only protocol to isolate the catalogue in those confirmation cells. E13 is the relevant current-board/current-victory study. Legacy `InvestT4` and `Siege:4` names label paired policy intent: final implementations clamp investment to available tier 3; that former InvestT4 cell therefore becomes a seed-paired T3-policy mirror rather than an independent T4 strategy. Frozen topology/map-study results were not regenerated.

The baseline is source `3b0dfed`, including Cleave and later bug fixes, not merely the older cleave worktree. `baseline-v1.3.json` pins all 24 pre-cut profiles. Existing JSON counters retain an empty tier-4 bucket to keep historical result readers compatible; this is telemetry schema, not a buildable unit. The standalone replay viewer retains fourth-tier labels solely to read frozen historical replays. Historical balance prototypes and result files are labeled historical rather than rewritten as if their measurements used 18 units.
