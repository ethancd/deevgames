# Economy and counterplay balance — v2.3

The designer authorized these changes and deployment after human playtesting.

| Elements | T1 purchase | T2 total | T3 total | T1 → T2 | T2 → T3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Fire, Lightning | 3 | 7 | 15 | 4 | 8 |
| Water, Shadow | 4 | 8 | 16 | 4 | 8 |
| Plant, Metal | 5 | 9 | 17 | 4 | 8 |

Only T1 can be purchased. Higher catalogue costs encode cumulative investment;
the existing cost-difference promotion rule yields universal 4/8 steps.

Muju defense increases from 2 to 3: Radi no longer kills it in one attack,
while Hi still does. Tanka defense decreases from 6 to 5: Kagari is the only
catalogue unit that can kill a full-health Tanka in one attack. Hi plus Radi
can now kill Tanka with combined attacks during the same exposure window.

C4, C5, H6 and H7 each decrease from 8 to 4 crystals. The new map total is
504, with 16 empty, 52 ordinary, 12 shelf and 20 rich squares. Rotation remains
symmetric. Starting income, passive mining, upkeep, six shared actions,
repeat movement, Cleave and placement timing remain unchanged.

Intent: give Plant better mining per purchase, make promotions accessible,
reduce disposable Radi assassination of miners and encourage earlier relocation.
These are playtest hypotheses, not a claim of solved balance.

## Existing games

Schema 5 remains compatible. Saved games and persistent online rooms retain
their stored reserves and initial capacities; no crystals already mined are
rewritten and no rooms are deleted. New games use the 504-crystal map. All games
use the current catalogue prices and stats on loading; completed purchases are
not repriced. Historical balance studies retain their original data.

## Validation

All 578 existing rules, AI/WASM, persistence, HTTP multiplayer and MCP tests pass.
Four additional combat cases pass for Radi/Hi versus Muju, the complete-catalogue
Tanka one-shot threshold, and a Hi/Radi combined kill. Server TypeScript checking
and the full three-game build with public-file verification pass. Existing browser
fixtures are updated for the new prices and surviving-target Cleave scenario.
