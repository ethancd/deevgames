# Phasing — experimental ruleset

> **Superseded 2026-09-21 by `SPEC.md` v3.1**, which states these rules
> normatively as the sole ruleset. Standard was retired on that date. This file
> is retained unchanged below as the dated record of the variant era; it is not
> current authority. Where it and SPEC v3.1 differ in framing — "variant",
> "Standard remains the default", "Built-in AI remains Standard-only", the
> schema-7 save note — SPEC v3.1 is current and this text is history.

Standard remains the default, with unchanged prices, units, map, actions and turn
semantics. It remains the benchmark for Hard AI development. Phasing is an
explicit, immutable match choice for Pass & Play, online rooms, and manual
analysis. Built-in AI remains Standard-only; MCP strategic analysis models both rulesets;
Phasing observations, legal actions, previews and recorded analysis work normally.

## Complete turn

Both players begin their first turn in Act, including Black with starting crystals.

1. At subsequent own turn starts, resolve existing home-occupation/elimination
   wins first, then check all own pending summons against the same board. A
   square must be empty and supported by any current unblocked spawn rectangle.
   Valid summons become actual tier-1 pieces. Invalid summons disappear and
   refund their exact original cost. Incoming pieces cannot support one another.
   Heal/reset the owner's actual pieces and grant four shared actions.
2. Act: move and attack under the existing rules. Arrivals can act immediately.
3. Mine & prepare: collect passive mining once, then pay upkeep. Income from this
   turn is available. Affordable upkeep is automatic unless review is enabled;
   otherwise choose an affordable keep-set. Tier 1 remains mandatory and free.
4. Prepare: promote actual pieces once per turn, including this turn's arrivals,
   and/or pay for public tier-1 summons by choosing their types and squares.
   Promotions apply immediately, but actions and mining have already finished.
   Their new upkeep rate applies after mining next own turn.
5. End turn: advance the kill-free clock once, resolve any draw, and hand over.

The server retains `turn.phase='place'` for Prepare. `END_ACTION_PHASE` collects
mining and settles upkeep, without handing over. `END_PLACE_PHASE` ends a Phasing
turn. One online time allowance covers the full turn; a phase change never resets
it. Preparation always ends explicitly, even when nothing is affordable.

## Pending summons

Commitments store owner, unit type, square, original paid cost and a unique ID.
All information is public. They are not actual units: they cannot occupy squares,
block movement, attack, be attacked, mine, promote, anchor or block rectangles,
occupy home, incur upkeep, or postpone elimination. One own commitment per square;
real units may move through or stop on the square. Only the arrival-time position
matters. A temporary intrusion is harmless if it has left before that check.

There is no relocation on failure. The refund is available during the arrival
turn, including its upkeep/preparation, with no automatic replacement purchase.
Ordinary within-turn undo can revise a commitment before handoff.

An invading piece must survive its own end-of-action upkeep before Phasing can
award immediate home-checkmate. The defender's rescue uses its actual army and
four actions, without pre-action promotions or upkeep releases. Home occupation
blocks every supporting rectangle, including all pending arrivals. The normal
next-own-turn home-occupation victory and previous-occupation priority remain.

## Presentation and compatibility

Setup, match headers, room invitations/lobbies and public observations identify
the ruleset. Dashed silhouettes mark pending pieces; the summon list shows type,
square, cost, stats and current support. Occupation leaves the dashed ring visible.
Arrival and full-refund notices, score events, instant replay and analysis retain
the commitments and rules identity.

Local save schema 7 reads schemas 5 and 6; missing ruleset means Standard. Restart
retains the match's chosen rules. New Phasing rooms use `muju-phasing-1`; Standard
rooms keep `muju-online-4`, including existing games. No room or saved board is
converted to Phasing. Keep ruleset identity in future benchmark manifests, packed
state/hash formats, opening corpora and strength comparisons before adapting AI.

## Validation

Focused tests cover phase order, income/upkeep timing, arrival/promotion, occupancy,
rectangle disruption and alternative anchors, non-chaining arrival validation,
refunds, elimination/home rules, quiet clock, undo/resume, server restart, atomic
commands, online clock handoff, public history and MCP analysis phase timing and unsupported built-in AI.
Browser tests exercise local desktop/phone play, resume, arrival-turn promotion,
online creation, observers and recorded analysis. This is correctness and usability
verification, not evidence of balance or a calibrated AI for Phasing.

## Revision note — 2026-09-19: `muju-phasing-2`, the quiet clock is twenty plies

Appended, not rewritten: everything above describes the variant as it stood under
`muju-phasing-1` and stays accurate except for the number below.

The inactivity draw fires after **20** consecutive complete quiet plies, not ten,
and the public counter turns amber at **17**, not seven. "Complete turn" step 5
("advance the kill-free clock once, resolve any draw, and hand over") is unchanged,
as is what resets the clock: only an attack that removes a unit. Summoning,
arrival, refunds, promotion, mining and upkeep still do not reset it.

Why it matters here specifically: under the old ten-ply clock a public committed
summon could be paid for, survive a turn, arrive, and still never influence a
result, because two players who both declined the first trade drew in five turns
each. Twenty plies lets the variant's delayed summons actually land and matter.
See `../JUDGMENT_LOG.md` J-021 and amendment A4 of
`hard-ai/PHASING-PREREGISTRATION-2026-09-18.md`.

Rules identity: new Phasing rooms use `muju-phasing-2`. A room stored under
`muju-phasing-1` is not silently reinterpreted under the longer clock: its row is
left untouched and it takes the existing `RULES_CHANGED` path, so no stored game is
replayed under a limit its players did not agree to. A local save below schema 8 is
adjudicated once under the limit it was recorded with and then resumes with a
restarted clock; `../SPEC.md` §9 states that rule and `../ONLINE.md` is
authoritative for rooms. Completed results stay final.

Benchmark manifests, packed state and hash formats, opening corpora and strength
comparisons recorded under `muju-phasing-1` are not pooled with `muju-phasing-2`
evidence. The opening books are unaffected: by their stop rule no opening carries
a clock value the two limits treat differently.
