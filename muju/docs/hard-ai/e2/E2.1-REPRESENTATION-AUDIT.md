# E2.1 — Representation audit

Which legal turn families the production generator can express, and how often the
missing ones matter. Measured 2026-09-16 on `claude/hard-ai-e2-lane3`.

EPIC-PLAN §2 records the finding this slice tests: "`gen/generate.ts` combines
purchases with at most one promotion — a legal family is absent in both narrow
and reference generation."

**Headline.** Two or more promotions are jointly legal and affordable in 50.0 %
of the analysed E1.1 loss roots (14 of 28), 9.0 % of exam `dev` roots and 2.0 %
of a 200-position corpus sample. The production generator emitted zero such
turns anywhere. Under the engine's own static evaluation the multi-promotion
turn beats the best one-promotion turn in 1 of 31 comparisons place-only, and
0 of 31 once the action phase is played out. The family is real and absent; the
shipped depth-0 evaluation gives no reason to add it.

## 1. The taxonomy

`lab/hard-ai/audit/families.ts`. Deterministic, decided entirely by
`src/ai/simulate.ts applyAction`; no engine state is consulted.

| place shape | meaning | `buildCombos` can hold it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `none` | no buy, no promotion | yes |
| `buys-only` | one or more `BUY_UNIT`, no promotion | yes |
| `promo-1` | exactly one `PROMOTE_UNIT`, no buy | yes |
| `promo-multi` | two or more promotions, no buy | **no** |
| `buy+promo-1` | at least one buy and exactly one promotion | yes |
| `buy+promo-multi` | at least one buy and two or more promotions | **no** |

`PlaceCombo` holds one purchase-plan index and one `promo` index. The two
`*-multi` shapes have no representation in that record at all.

Tags cut across the shapes: `upkeep-release` (paid upkeep and released at least
one body, named by the released definitions), `summon-strike` (a body bought
this turn moved or attacked), `summon-attack`, `home-entry`, `kill`, `win`. The
place-family key carries the upkeep choice, the shape, the bought definitions
and the pre-promotion definitions; squares are not part of it, because the
generator's product removes shapes, not squares.

**Summon-and-strike needs no substitute definition.** `building.ts:13` mints a
bought body with `canActThisTurn: true`, and `src/game/types.ts:77` states the
rule outright: "placement and promotion do not impose summoning sickness".
`placedThisTurn` blocks only the PROMOTION of a body bought this turn
(`promotion.ts:46`), never its actions.

Unit tests: `tests/lab/audit-families.test.ts`, 10 tests.

## 2. The canonical place-phase enumerator

`lab/hard-ai/audit/place-enum.ts`.

Every prefix is built by applying real `AIAction`s through the canonical
simulator, one after another, so joint affordability, once-per-unit,
never-on-the-turn-bought and the tier cap are enforced by the rules. Individually
legal promotions are never concatenated. The walk is BREADTH FIRST by prefix
length: a depth-first walk spends its whole budget inside the first promotion's
subtree and then reports "no buys-only prefix" for a position full of them.

Two square modes. `squares: 'all'` tries every buy on every legal spawn square.
`squares: 'first-legal'` is the default and the mode every table below uses:
each bought definition takes the first legal spawn square in canonical order.
A square changes neither price, upkeep, tier nor promotability, so the FAMILY
set is unchanged, except where one placement blocks a square another buy in the
same prefix needed; `spawn.tight` records when a position is close to that
condition (43.7 % here), so the abstraction is declared, not assumed away.

Bounds: `maxVisits` 200,000 node expansions, `maxPrefixes` 20,000 prefixes,
`maxUpkeepBranches` 24 keep-sets, `maxBuys` 4. The buy bound matches
`gen/purchase.ts PURCHASE_MAX_BODIES` so the comparison against the production
shortlist is like for like; the rules allow more.

**What `capped` means.** A bound ran out; the prefix count and every family
count are then LOWER BOUNDS, and "no prefix with two promotions was found" stops
being evidence that none exists. Nothing is estimated past a cap. 4 of 373
positions capped (1.1 %).

The production side is measured, not inferred. `lab/hard-ai/audit/gen-view.ts`
drives the real `TurnGenerator` the way `search/pvs.ts generateAt` drives it at
ply 0 — same `GenConfig`, `proverMode = 2`, level-2 tables, same within-turn
scorer, `installRescueWitness`, `UNLIMITED_WORK` as the meter — and decodes each
emitted `Turn` with the engine's own `decodeTurn`.

## 3. Measurements

373 positions: 28 loss roots (the first-consequential and largest-swing turns of
the 14 analysed E1.1 losses, reconstructed with `analyze/replay.ts reconstruct`),
145 exam `dev` roots, and 200 corpus positions from a seeded shuffle, `--seed=1`.

### 3.1 How often the missing family is legal

| quantity | losses (28) | exam (145) | corpus (200) | all (373) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| bank > 0 | 27 (96.4 %) | 47 (32.4 %) | 88 (44.0 %) | 162 (43.4 %) |
| at least one affordable promotion | 21 (75.0 %) | 24 (16.6 %) | 29 (14.5 %) | 74 (19.8 %) |
| two promotions jointly affordable at the root | 14 (50.0 %) | 9 (6.2 %) | 4 (2.0 %) | 27 (7.2 %) |
| a legal place prefix with >= 2 promotions | 14 (50.0 %) | 13 (9.0 %) | 4 (2.0 %) | 31 (8.3 %) |
| enumeration capped | 3 | 1 | 0 | 4 |

The two bottom rows differ on the exam set because the affordability estimate is
read at the root. At an `upkeepPending` root the bodies still carry last turn's
`placedThisTurn` / `promotedThisPlacement` flags, which `turn.ts finishTurnStart`
clears through `resetUnitActions` when the upkeep is paid, so more bodies become
promotable after `PAY_UPKEEP` than the root count sees. The enumerated row is
authoritative. The corpus number is low because that corpus is mostly authored
tactical positions and opening snapshots with an empty bank; the loss number is
the one that describes real play.

### 3.2 Prefixes, families, and what the generator emitted

| quantity | value |
| --- | --- |
| legal place prefixes enumerated | 54,226 |
| of those, with >= 2 promotions | 36,346 (67.0 %) |
| of those, in a shape `buildCombos` cannot hold | 36,346 (67.0 %) |
| multi-promotion turns the production generator emitted | 0 |

Rows two and three are equal by construction: `promo-multi` and
`buy+promo-multi` are the only inexpressible shapes.

Distinct place families per position: 1 family for 262 positions, 2-4 for 40,
5-16 for 41, 17-64 for 18, 65+ for 12.

Shapes over all 7,112 turns the generator emitted across the 373 positions:
`none` 4,946; `buys-only` 1,815; `promo-1` 194; `buy+promo-1` 157;
`promo-multi` 0; `buy+promo-multi` 0.

### 3.3 The adviser's own choices

Adviser turns classified at the 28 loss roots: 28. In a shape the generator
cannot express: **0**. Played turns: 28, inexpressible: 0.

This is zero BY CONSTRUCTION, and it proves the blind spot is shared rather than
empty. `hard:analyze`'s adviser is the same `HardEngine` at a larger fixed work
budget, drawing candidates from the same `buildCombos`, so E1's
`candidate-absent` class could never have fired on this family. The E1 loss
classification is silent about it; that silence is not evidence against it.

### 3.4 A bounded static preference signal

Not strength. The score is the engine's own `Evaluator.stage0 + stage1` at ply 0
— the closure `analyze/engine.ts` and `recall/run.ts` both build for the
within-turn score — with no search, reply or quiescence. `place-only` scores the position the place phase leaves, over every prefix,
uncapped. `with action phase` plays the action phase out canonically from the
ten best prefixes of each group (ranked by place-only score) with a 6,000-visit
cap per prefix, and takes the best end.

| signal | comparable | >= 2 promotions wins | wins by > 300 cc | median delta | max delta |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| place-only, uncapped | 31 | 1 (3.2 %) | 1 (3.2 %) | -968 cc | +316 cc |
| with action phase, top-10, capped | 31 | 0 (0.0 %) | 0 (0.0 %) | -1,156 cc | 0 cc |

The single place-only winner is `fuzz-1000.jsonl#fuzz-5150-619-9`: +316 cc
place-only, -254 cc once the action phase is played out.

Caveats, all load-bearing. 30 of the 31 action-phase comparisons hit the visit
cap and 23 truncated the prefix list to ten, so both sides are lower bounds. The
evaluator is the same one that scores the generator's candidates, so a feature
set that does not value a second promotion will not prefer one here. The
place-only median of -968 cc mostly reports that two promotions leave less
material than two bodies. E2.3's equal-time contest is what can price the change.

## 4. Witness cases added

Four cases appended to `lab/hard-ai/exam/cases/dev.jsonl` by
`lab/hard-ai/audit/seed-cases.ts`. The label is decided by verification, never
asserted: `exact` requires that the canonical enumeration COMPLETED and that some
end position satisfying the claim is reachable ONLY through the case's family.
`complete` is false on those witnesses because the key list is the family-only
subset, not every end satisfying the claim.

| id | demand | family | label |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `e21-multi-promotion-double-threshold` | `immediate-action` | two promotions | exact — 33 winning ends need >= 2 promotions |
| `e21-summon-and-strike` | `immediate-action` | buy then strike | exact — 54 killing ends need a body bought this turn |
| `e21-purchase-plus-promotion` | `immediate-action` | buy + promotion | **judgment** — 95 winning ends need both, but the runner cannot re-verify it |
| `e21-upkeep-release-choice` | `recurring-upkeep` | upkeep release | exact — 68 killing ends, all behind a forced release |

The seeder re-verifies every exact witness a second time at the exam runner's
own `DEFAULT_ENUM_BUDGET` of 300,000 canonical calls and demotes anything that
budget cannot confirm; a case that is exact only under a seeding-sized budget is
not exact in practice. `e21-purchase-plus-promotion` is what that caught: a
12,000,000-call enumeration proves 95 of its 405 winning ends need both halves,
300,000 calls cannot re-derive it, and the case says so in its `reason`.
Shrinking that position until it fits the runner's budget is the follow-up.

The multi-promotion position puts both attackers on their own back rank, where
`getAllSpawnPositions` returns nothing, so no purchase can substitute for a
promotion, and each black body yields only to ITS OWN attacker's promoted form.
The attacker/victim pairs are found by search over the shipped price list, not
hand derived, because the element graph makes the threshold
direction-dependent.

## 5. Design note — the minimal change (NOT implemented here)

`src/ai/hard/gen/*` is lane 4's territory this session. Nothing below was
written to the engine.

**Shape.** `PlaceCombo` grows from `promo: number` to `promos:
Int32Array(MAX_COMBO_PROMOTIONS)` plus `promoCount`, and `buildCombos`
enumerates, per purchase plan, the promotion SUBSETS up to that width.
`runCombo` already applies actions one at a time through `Replica.isLegal` /
`make`, so it needs only to loop over `promos`.

**The four constraints.** Joint affordability: `buildCombos` accumulates
`plan.spend + sum(promo costs)` against `bank`; this matters because
`planPromotions` prices each candidate against the FULL root bank (`if (cost >
bank) continue`), so a subset of its output can be individually affordable and
jointly not. One per unit: enumerate subsets over distinct slots, and
`planPromotions` emits at most one candidate per slot. Never the unit bought
this turn: `planPromotions` skips `F_PLACED` and runs before the plan's buys are
applied, and `runCombo` applying buys before promotions keeps that true at
dispatch. Tier-3 cap: `cat.nextDef[def] < 0` already removes terminal tiers.

**Behind a config field.** `GenConfig` gains `maxComboPromotions`, default 1.
At 1 the subset enumeration must degenerate to today's `j = -1 .. promoCount-1`
loop and produce byte-identical combos, so `hard@desktop`'s resolved config hash
is unchanged by default and no existing arm moves. The experiment arm sets 2.

**Cost model.** Today's combo count is `1 + planCount x (promoCount + 1)`,
pruned to `maxPlacePlans` (16 at the root, 200 in the reference). At
`maxComboPromotions = 2` it becomes `1 + planCount x (1 + promoCount +
C(promoCount, 2))`: with `maxPromotions = 8`, 37 per plan instead of 9, a 4x
pool before pruning. The scoring loop grows, not the search — but the pressure
moves to `maxPlacePlans`, already the binding constraint at 16, so 28 extra
combos per plan displace purchase plans unless it rises, and `tuneKeep` divides
K by the plan count so more plans also narrows the action beam. That interaction
is the real cost and must be measured.

**Evidence to want before pricing it as an equal-time arm.** E2.2 coverage
instrumentation showing where a known-good multi-promotion turn is removed, on
the cases added here. A `hard:recall` run with `maxComboPromotions = 2` against
a reference that is ALSO widened, because today's K=2000 reference shares the
blind spot and relative recall against it cannot see the family. A rerun of §3.4
under reply search rather than depth-0; if the signal stays negative there, the
arm should not be built. Then an equal-time contest at 3,000 ms per turn, with
the combo growth paid out of the same budget.

## 8. Reproducing

```
cd muju
npm run hard:audit -- --sample=200 --seed=1
node --import tsx lab/hard-ai/audit/seed-cases.ts --write
npm run hard:types && npx tsc --noEmit
npx vitest run tests/lab tests/ai/hard && npm run hard:exam
```

`hard:audit` writes `lab/results/hard-ai-e2/audit/audit.json` and `audit.md`;
the run above took 271.5 s in one process. `--sources` selects the position
sets, `--no-gen` skips the production generator pass. The seeder takes about
8 minutes: its root enumerations run to 12,000,000 canonical calls.
