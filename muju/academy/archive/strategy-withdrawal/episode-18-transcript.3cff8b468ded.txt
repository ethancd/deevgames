The Missing Seventh Action

PIP: I dropped our seventh action somewhere.

CLICK: We never had a seventh action.

PIP: Then I have found the problem.

PIP: My plan. Two Hono walk in and the fortress comes down.

CLICK: Pip. Same pocket. A partly used action is still an action.

COACH: So each trip rounds up on its own. Leftovers do not carry.

COACH: The Hono has attack three. Add one against Metal. That makes four damage.

COACH: The Tanka has six defense, so it takes two hits.

CLICK: Leave it standing and that Hono is done. Two hits means two Hono.

COACH: The left Hono has five squares to walk.

COACH: At speed two, that is three movement actions. Then one attack. Four for the left Hono.

CLICK: Right Hono: three actions. Four and three. Seven. The tray holds six.

COACH: Enough damage is not enough actions. This plan does not fit.

CLICK: Blue flag. A different starting position.

COACH: The left Hono is already on B four. Its path to E five is four squares.

PIP: One square nearer. That can change the price of the walk.

COACH: Does the same plan fit in six actions now?

COACH: There is no hurry. Look as long as you want, or let me show you.

COACH: Yes. It fits.

COACH: Four squares cost two movement actions, then one attack. Three each. Six exactly.

PIP: We did not find another action. We stopped needing it.

CLANK: I moved the daisy one square last turn. Today it did not have to hurry.

PIP: So B four was free?

CLICK: B four was a turn. Somebody spent that action. Filed under the Missing Seventh.

COACH: Today's six add up. Whether yesterday's step was safe is its own count.

COACH: Now put a friend in that path. A friend in the hallway is still a wall.

CLICK: The walk goes around. Four squares still. Count it, do not guess it.

PIP: My plan survives a friend. I did not expect that.

COACH: Find the one action board two never has to spend. Then stop if you want.

PIP: We did not find another action. We stopped needing it.

PIP: The search party is dismissed. Nobody looks under the table again.
