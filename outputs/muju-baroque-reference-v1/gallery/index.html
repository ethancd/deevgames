The Turned Court

Eighteen elemental chess pieces. Six craft traditions. A common turned body gives each family room to speak through its crown, material and ornament.

Tier → height38 / 50 / 64 mm + rank pips
Defense → massWaist and shoulder breadth
Attack → edgeSoft seed to cutting crown
Speed → gestureRooted foot to open spiral
Mining → channelsRoots, fluting and collection

White material studies · Dimensions are sculpting targets; portraits are not to a common scale. Exact stats below match the current Muju catalogue. Ferocity describes the design, not an additional game mechanic.

Design definition
