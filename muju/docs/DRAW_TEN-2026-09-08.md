# Ten quiet turns, resolved at turn end — v1.9

The user requested a draw immediately at the end of the quiet turn and proposed shortening the limit to ten player turns. The new rule is ten consecutive completed quiet player turns (five full rounds), resolved before the next player starts. Mining at least one crystal or eliminating an enemy by attack resets the counter immediately; that productive turn ends at zero. Other activity still does not reset it.

A home occupation cannot override a draw at the previous turn end. A home win still resolves normally if the next turn begins below the limit. Eliminating the last enemy during a turn still wins immediately. At a draw, no next-turn upkeep, queue advancement, healing, player switch or round increment occurs.

## Design assessment

There is a tradeoff: maneuvering, building and promotions can be purposeful without satisfying the progress definition. A ten-turn limit can truncate a genuine plan and increases the value of retaining an accessible crystal or forcing an attack elimination. Position repetition and a no-progress timer constrain different things. This is an intentional pace rule, not a proof that every quiet game is dead.

The historical E13 v1.6 upkeep sample had 805 natural wins out of 960 games; none reached ten quiet turns. Without upkeep/draw, 4 of 700 natural wins reached 10 or more (maximum streaks 11, 12, 21, 21). The 260 safety-capped control games are excluded from natural wins. These are older bots and stats, not an expert-play study or a paired test of the new threshold. Per-game maxima do not establish counts of multiple quiet streaks. See the reproducible raw-record summary in `lab/results/draw-ten-2026-09-08/historical-screen.json`.

Given the preference for shorter games, this screen supplies no strong objection to ten. Watch for meaningful maneuvering being cut off in future human play rather than preserving twenty solely as a precaution.
