One Turn at the Practice Table
Rules v2.2 · Video version 5 · Last updated at 21:39 Sep 10 2026 CDT

PIP: My checklist. One box. Do the turn correctly.

CLICK: Pay. Place. Act. Mine.

PIP: Four things. Then I am issuing three more boxes. With names.

CLICK: Filed by Pip. Pay this morning's bill with this evening's payday.

COACH: Good to see you, Pip. Overruled. This morning's bill needs crystals already in the bank.

PIP: My promise was perfectly good. It was just facing backwards.

COACH: First box. Pay the bills. A big piece pays a toll when our own turn starts.

CLICK: The Hono owes one. The Muju owes nothing. Bank five. Now bank four.

COACH: Second box. The shop. A Hi at C three costs two crystals and no actions.

PIP: Then the bank says four.

CLICK: Two.

PIP: The box was right. My math needed the box.

COACH: Third box. This trip: one action.

COACH: That trip was one square. A Hi may walk two squares for the same action.

COACH: Five actions are unused, and that is allowed. The last box is the one we count together.

CLICK: The Hono. Mining one. One crystal still sitting in that square.

CLICK: The Muju. Mining three. Three crystals still sitting in that square.

CLICK: The new Hi. Mining one. Its square is empty.

COACH: At our turn end, how much income arrives?

COACH: Take as long as you like. Or watch it happen with me.

COACH: Four crystals arrived.

CLICK: Bank two. Now bank six.

COACH: Even a new piece that moved can collect. This Hi took nothing because its square was empty.

PIP: I have ticked every box. The board declined to congratulate me.

COACH: Now look at tomorrow morning's bill. The Hono still owes one.

PIP: So tonight's crystals can pay tomorrow's toll.

COACH: They can. They were never allowed to pay this morning's.

COACH: Take the mission card. Play that turn twice, then meet me at the Toast Department.

CLICK: Pay. Place. Act. Mine.

COACH: Pay the bills first. Mine crystals last. Stopping here is fine.
