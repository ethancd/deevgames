PIP: My opening plan. The word on it is win. Very large letters.

COACH: What does it ask you to do first?

PIP: Win. It is more of a poster than a plan.

CLICK: From the drawer. Pay today's bill with tomorrow's crystals. Filed three times.

PIP: I believe in it.

CLICK: Overruled three times. Crystals arrive at the end. The shop opens at the start.

COACH: This is the real beginning. Three pieces each. Our Muju is on A two.

CLICK: Both banks hold zero crystals. Place is skipped. We start with actions.

COACH: Plan one walks our Sjor to B four. That forward friend is an anchor.

COACH: Next turn, if that rectangle stays clear, the shop can deliver to an empty square inside it.

PIP: A forward friend could get knocked over. A very brief anchor.

COACH: Plan two, then.

COACH: Buy a friend now and stand it somewhere safe.

CLICK: A Hi costs two crystals. The bank holds zero.

COACH: Whatever our pieces collect arrives at the end of the turn.

COACH: Can we buy that Hi now, using the payday we expect at the end?

COACH: No clock on this. Or watch the shelf with me.

COACH: Not today. The reason is about when things happen.

COACH: Place was skipped. Payday comes after actions.

COACH: It cannot reach backwards. Plan one is an option, not the only legal move.

CLICK: Crystals expected: some. Crystals in the bank: zero. Filed a fourth time.

CLICK: Pay. Place. Act. Mine.

COACH: The bill is zero. Place is skipped. Six actions and a payday.

COACH: Three questions for the squares we stop on. Can they earn?

PIP: Can they help us put a friend down next turn?

COACH: And can Black hit back at that square? Pip asked that one first.

CLICK: B four just turned red. New threat. New plan. Same three questions.

PIP: My poster folds into a card. It lives in the drawer now.

PIP: New threat. New plan. Same three questions. I put that on the front.

COACH: Ask whoever plays with you: which square would you protect next turn?
