The previously inspected Codex–Claude Phasing snapshot (revision 32, after White round 16) had three Metal purchases out of 47. Combining that snapshot with these five human matches gives 3/140 purchases, with Metal present in only 1/6 matches. The Claude game snapshot is unfinished; do not treat these six as six completed matches.

Interpretation: Metal was entirely absent from the human-match sample, so its practical relevance in those games is an open design concern. This establishes non-use, not that Metal would have been a bad choice. The players repeatedly chose other elements; evaluating missed opportunities requires counterfactual position analysis. The standard-game evidence should not be used to dismiss the Phasing-specific concern.
