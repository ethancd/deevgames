## 1. A13-1 — `rootDiagnostic: true` on every `weights` and `evalFix` arm

Commit `e169ccd1`. Amendment `amendments/lane13.md` A13-1.

### The fact that changed

- The flag's meaning: `false` says the recall instrument cannot see this arm,
  so its ROOT columns are `base`'s by construction and reading them as
  agreement is a mistake.
- It was `false` on the 9 `weights` arms and the `evalFix` arms because
  `lab/hard-ai/recall/run.ts` built its own `allocTables()` and its own
  `new Evaluator(this.rep)`, carrying neither the arm's weight vector nor its
  `evalFix` block. Under `--arm eval-no-safety` the instrument measured
  `DEFAULT_WEIGHTS`, i.e. `base`, and the run was an A/A.
- Lane 13's A8 / L5-A1 fix ended that: `recallEnginePatch(arm)` resolves both
  and stamps them on the evaluator and on both `NodeTables`.
- Lane 13's measurement, 20 root + 10 reply positions of `fuzz-1000.jsonl`
  (`E3.3-INSTRUMENT-FOLLOWUPS.md` §2): `eval-no-safety` `top1` .30 -> .35,
  `replyTop1` .20 -> .30, `regret_p90` 1,432 -> 931 cc; `eval-correct-v1`
  `top3` .50 -> .45, `regret_p90` 1,432 -> 1,222 cc. The columns move, so the
  flag was false about the arms.

### What was edited

- 16 arms, selected by FACTOR, not by name: the 9 whose factor is `weights`
  (`eval-no-economy`, `eval-no-home`, `eval-no-safety`, `eval-no-space`,
  `eval-no-invariants`, `eval-stage01`, and lane 11's `eval-no-threat-stack`,
  `eval-no-anchor`, `eval-no-safety-inv`) and the 7 whose factor is `evalFix`
  (`eval-fix-b1` through `eval-fix-b6` and `eval-correct-v1`).
- lane13.md says FIFTEEN. It was written before lane 14 added `eval-fix-b6`,
  so the count there is superseded by sixteen; nothing else in A13-1 changes.
- The 6 arms that keep `rootDiagnostic: false` are `reply-wide`,
  `interior-action-wide`, `interior-place-wide`, `calib`, `work-fit` and
  `work-fit-deep` — the interior-only and allocation arms, for which the flag
  is still exactly true.
- THREE comment blocks gave the stale reason, not two: the `weights` reason is
  spelled out once for lane 5's six group arms and again for lane 11's three
  sub-arms, and the `evalFix` reason once for lane 12's arms. All three are
  rewritten; lane13.md's "two" counts reasons, not sites.
- The napkin's E3 entry records why this was not a bulk replace: a
  registry-wide `replace('rootDiagnostic: false', …)` flipped 25 arms, E1.3's
  interior arms included, and broke three tests. The edit here filtered on the
  `factor:` line above each flag and rewrote the tests in the same commit.

### Test

passing):

- `marks the arms a ROOT recall item cannot see, and no longer counts the
  weights and evalFix arms among them` — the invisible list is the six

- `says an interior-only arm cannot move a root column, and says it of the

- `records the two amendments as APPLIED: the arms are root-diagnostic and the

## 3. `summary.md` lists the openings the run played

Commit `83e34ad2`. Source: `E3.2-ROW-REPORT.md` §10.5.

### The bug

- Rows #1 and #2 both printed `openings: used 16 of 32` and then all 32 ids of
  the post-skip POOL.
- `metrics.json.openingsUsed` and `manifest.openings.used` held the correct 16
  throughout, so no result was wrong and no decision moved.
- A reader of `summary.md` alone over-counted the run's independence width by a
  factor of two, and A15 makes that width the thing the interval rests on.

### What was edited

- `summaryMarkdown`'s openings line lists `metrics.openingsUsed` instead of
  `metrics.openings`. The `used N of M` count is untouched, so the pool size is
  still on the line, and the pool itself is still listed in full in
  `metrics.json.openings` and `manifest.openings.ids` — which is what
  `--resume` compares, so nothing identity-bearing changed.
- Schema: unchanged. No field added or removed; this is a rendering fix.

### Test

`tests/lab/ladder-runner.test.ts`:

- `prints 'used N of M' on the summary openings line and lists the USED ids,
  not the pool` — over the 14-opening / 16-pair fixture the line reads
  `used 8 of 14 — o0 … o7`, the six unreached ids are absent, and the listed
  ids are asserted equal to `metrics.openingsUsed`.
- `writes a summary.md whose openings line lists the ids the run PLAYED, not
  the pool it drew from` — a real 1-pair Rush-vs-Rush ladder over a 2-opening
  pool, with `summary.md`, `metrics.json` and `manifest.json` read off disk:
  the summary says `used 1 of 2 — o0`, and `o1` is still in

## 4. Per-game box load

Commit `fe7ac364`. Source: critique C8, and C13 as the row report §5 and
§11 record it.

### The gap

- E2's re-pin row held slot 0 while E3's row #1 held slot 1, with lane 11's
  fixed-work row queued and lanes 9/10/12 probing; loadavg was 3.3 at launch
  and 6.6-9.0 at 06:40Z.
- Within-row fairness was never in question: both seats play in one process
  under one clock, so load lowers both alike, and the units/ms columns record
  it.
- What load does affect is the comparison ACROSS rows. The row report had to
  record row #1's and row #2's rung distributions as not comparable with E2's
  for exactly this reason.
- The only load an artifact held was `manifest.loadavg`: one sample, taken
  before the first game of a run that lasted 7,486,573 ms.

### What was edited

- `lab/hard-ai/ladder/worker.ts`: `GameRow.loadAvgStart` and
  `GameRow.loadAvgEnd`, both `os.loadavg()[0]` (the 1-minute average), sampled
  immediately before the game's first turn and immediately after its last.
- Both fields are OPTIONAL: additive to the `games.jsonl` schema, so a row
  written before this landed still parses and a merge of one old and one new
  shard still computes.
- `lab/hard-ai/ladder/run.ts`: `RunMetrics.loadAvgMean` is the mean of every
  `loadAvgStart` and `loadAvgEnd` in the merged games, and `summary.md` prints
  it.
- A run carrying no samples reports `null` and the summary says
  "not recorded (run predates the per-game samples)". It never reports 0: a 0
  reads as an idle box, which is the opposite of the fact.

### Test

`tests/lab/ladder-runner.test.ts`, three cases:

- `averages every loadAvgStart and loadAvgEnd in games.jsonl into
  metrics.loadAvgMean` — samples 2, 4, 6, 8 over two games give 5.00, and the
  summary line carries it.
- `reports a run whose rows predate the fields as NULL, never as an idle box` —
  `null` with the "not recorded" line, and a half-instrumented merge (one old
  row, one new) averages the two samples it has.
- `writes both samples on every real game row, and the run mean into
  metrics.json` — a real 1-pair Rush-vs-Rush run, with `games.jsonl`,
  `metrics.json` and `summary.md` read off disk and the mean recomputed from
  the four samples.

### What this does NOT do

- It does not retrofit the rows already played. Rows #1, #2 and #3 and E2's
  re-pin carry no per-game samples, and they are not re-run; their reports keep
  the "not comparable across rows" wording.
- It does not make loads comparable by itself. A future report that compares
  two rows' rung distributions must quote both rows' `loadAvgMean` beside them.
- It does not touch the two EPIC-PLAN §5 columns §10.9 records as NOT MEASURED
  (peak memory, p99 turn latency, and startup/packing/verification overhead).
