Measured (printed by the instrument from `GAMMA_Q16` itself, not typed in).

- `pstCoeffCc[t]` = value in cc that one crystal mined on own-turn `t`
  contributes to `PST_MINE` (`core/income.ts:50-69`), `t = 1..12`:
  90.00, 81.00, 72.90, 65.61, 59.05, 53.14, 47.83, 43.05, 38.74, 34.87, 31.38,
  28.24.
- `streamCoeffCc[t]` = what the same crystal contributes to
  `EconResult.stream` (`tables/economy.ts:221`), `t = 1..6`:
  81.00, 72.90, 65.61, 59.05, 53.14, 47.83. Zero for `t ≥ 7`: the economy DP's
  horizon is `ECON_HORIZON = 6` (`tables/economy.ts:29`) while the PST's is
  `PST_HORIZON = 12` (`core/income.ts:30`).
- `Σ_{t=1..6} pstCoeffCc[t] = 421.70`. `RENT_PV` is 422
  (`core/income.ts:47`). Reading: `RENT_PV` is exactly the present value of one
  crystal per turn of rent over six turns at γ = 0.9, rounded up by 0.3 cc.
- Reading: `stream` discounts own-turn `t` by `GAMMA_Q16[t+1]`
  (`tables/economy.ts:221`, `stream += GAMMA_Q16[turnIdx + 2] × …`), so the
  economy DP's first turn is discounted by γ² = 0.81 while `PST_MINE`'s first
  turn is discounted by γ¹ = 0.90. The two tables disagree about what "next
  turn" is worth by one factor of γ.

## Ledger 3 — one crystal per turn of upkeep

Readings:

- `Rent` = `Σ upkeep[def]` difference, `features.ts:175`, w **−422**
  (`weights.ts:57`), and `RENT_PV = 422` is the six-turn γ = 0.9 present value
  of one crystal of rent per turn (`core/income.ts:47`, arithmetic above).
- `EconDelta`'s stream is `Σ γ^{t+1} × (incomeₜ − upkeepₜ) × 100`
  (`tables/economy.ts:221`), and `upkeepₜ` is the SAME standing bill on every
  one of the six turns (`tables/economy.ts:175`, `upkeepPerTurn` is computed
  once outside the loop). One crystal per turn of upkeep therefore subtracts
  `Σ_{t=1..6} γ^{t+1} × 100 = 379.53 cc` from `stream`, which `EconDelta`
  carries at w = 80 after `div100`: **−303.6 cc**.

So one crystal per turn of upkeep costs **−725.6 cc** derived, against the
`RENT_PV` of 422 the design states for it. The rent stream is priced twice over
the same six-turn horizon, shifted by one turn: **1.72 × RENT_PV**.

## Ledger 4 — the miner purchase: is the investment double-credited?

Reading: no. `Material` values the body (`cost × 100`, DESIGN F9) and
`PstMine`/`EconDelta` value the crystals the body will take; they are disjoint
quantities. The triple count EPIC-PLAN §4 warns about — bank, projected mining,
finite reserves, all of the same future crystals — does not occur:

- bank crystals are counted by `BankLiquid`/`BankExcess`/`BankConvertible`;
- crystals under a miner are counted by `PstMine` and `EconDelta`;
- crystals in the ground inside my spawn rectangle are counted by
  `SpawnReserve`, and `core/spawn.ts:99` restricts that sum to *empty* squares,
  so a square is never in both.

What is wrong is not the count but the coefficient, and it is wrong in both
directions at once (Ledger 2): +0.52 γᵗ on the first six turns, −0.20 γᵗ on the
next six.

Measured, position A, reversing the purchase (`plant_1` removed, its 5 crystals
refunded): score **−4070 cc**, so the evaluator pays 4070 cc to own the Muju
rather than the cash. That perturbation is heavily confounded on a single-unit
fixture — removing the only unit takes the spawn rectangle to zero and fires
`SpawnZero` −800 and `Inv1SpawnZero` −800 as well as `SpawnArea` −450,
`AnchorDepth` −150, `AnchorFragility` −240, `BlockingDeficit` −300 — so the
only clean reading from it is the economy sub-total: `Material` −500,
`PstMine` −660, `EconDelta` +80, `DepletionWaste` +60, bank terms +190.

Horizon versus SU §1 payback (judge 3):

- SU §1.7's plant line collects 16 crystals in three harvests for 5 + 4 + 8 =
  17 crystals and 1 rent during the climb. The evaluator's own view of the same
  trade is Ledger 3's −1282 cc for the first step alone.
- SU §1.2's schedules say a Muju empties a 16-cell in six turns. `ECON_HORIZON`
  = 6 covers exactly that, and `PST_HORIZON` = 12 covers twice it. For any
  miner slower than 3/turn on a rich cell — Hi at 1/turn takes 16 turns, Sjor
  at 2/turn takes 8 — the second half of the yield lands in the window where
  the evaluator's coefficient is negative.

## Ledger 5 — `DepletionWaste` and `RelocationDebt` against SU §1.4

Readings:

- `waste = Σ (mine − take)` over every miner-turn of the horizon
  (`tables/economy.ts:204`, `EconResult.waste` doc at `:46`). A miner whose cell
  is dry accrues its full mining rate as waste on every remaining turn, so a dry
  Muju (mine 3) reaches waste 18 over six turns and costs **−540 cc**
  (w = −30, `weights.ts:82`) — more than the Muju's 500 cc of material.
- The same forgone income is already absent from `EconDelta`'s stream, which
  only ever adds what was actually taken. The shortfall is therefore booked
  twice: once as income that does not appear, once as waste that does.
- `waste` rises when a miner mines FASTER. Position A measured: `plant_1`
  (mine 3) on the 16-cell has waste 2 and `plant_2` (mine 5) has waste 14, so
  the promotion costs **−360 cc** of `DepletionWaste` while extracting the same
  16 crystals. Judge 3, SU §1.2: "Lifetime yield of a square to a miner is
  `min(reserve, …)` = the reserve itself; the rate only decides *how many turns*
  the miner is tied up." The champion charges the miner for being tied up for
  fewer turns.
- `RelocationDebt` = `econ.relocationDebt / 100` at w = −60
  (`features.ts:421`, `weights.ts:85`), and `relocationDebt += actionCost ×
  ACTION_VALUE_CC` with `ACTION_VALUE_CC = 60` (`tables/economy.ts:31, :210`).
  Under the crystals-not-centi-crystals convention (`features.ts:11-16`) the
  score charge is `−60 × (actionCost × 60) / 100 = −36 cc per action`, not the
  60 cc per action DESIGN §4.12 declares. The same action is worth +40 cc as
  tempo (`ActionsLeft`, `weights.ts:76`).
- `relocationDebt` is charged undiscounted at the root while the income it buys
  is discounted by γ^{t+1}, and it is charged for relocations the DP merely
  plans inside a projection. Measured at position B: White 420 cc (7 actions),
  Black 900 cc (15 actions), feature −4, contribution +240 cc to White.

The dry-cell cliff SU §1.4 describes is therefore priced, but not as SU
describes it. SU §1.4 says income after the cliff is decided by how many miners
you can *walk* onto fresh stacks within 4 AP. The champion instead pays a
standing penalty for holding a miner whose cell is thin (`DepletionWaste`,
up to −540 cc per dry Muju) and a discounted-benefit, undiscounted-cost charge
for the walk (`RelocationDebt`, 36 cc per action).

### The relocation trigger is not monotone in reserve

Measured, position B, one resource layer ADDED under White's `water_1` at B2
(x 1, y 1), reserve 2 → 3, mining rate 2:

- Score **−130 cc**: `PstMine` +60, `EconDelta` −160, `DepletionWaste` −30.
- White's projected income sequence moved from `[7,4,2,7,3,2]` (25 crystals) to
  `[7,5,0,7,5,0]` (**24** crystals), and `stream` fell from 1655 to 1608, after
  a crystal was added to the board.
- Reading of the mechanism: `tables/economy.ts:206` triggers a relocation only
  when `take === 0`, i.e. only when the cell is completely dry. The extra
  crystal lets the miner take 1 on turn 2 instead of going dry, so the
  relocation starts one turn later and the whole remaining schedule shifts.
- Judge 4, canonical fact: the board strictly gained a crystal and no unit,
  square or rule changed. A projection whose total falls when the stock rises
  is describing something the rules do not contain.

## Findings, ranked by expected cost to play

The ranking is by how often the term fires and how large it is, on the two
positions measured and on the feature definitions. It is not an Elo estimate.

1. **Rent is charged twice, at 1.72 × `RENT_PV`.** −725.6 cc derived, −742 cc
   measured, per crystal per turn of upkeep. Fires on every position where
   either side owns a tier-2 or tier-3 body, which is every position past the
   opening. It taxes exactly the promotions SU §1.5 and §1.7 call
   income-positive.
2. **Mining past own-turn 6 is scored negative** at −0.20 γᵗ × 100 cc per
   crystal, measured −40 cc over crystals 7–12 of a mine-1 miner. Fires on
   every slow miner on a rich cell — the assignment SU §1.2 calls "the whole
   economic game".
3. **`economyDP` is not monotone in reserve.** Adding one crystal under a
   miner cost 130 cc and lowered projected six-turn income by one crystal at
   position B. Fires wherever a miner sits on a thin, non-empty cell, which is
   the post-cliff regime of SU §1.4 and §6.3.
4. **`DepletionWaste` penalises mining speed and idle capacity, double-booking
   against the stream.** −360 cc for the SU §1.7 promotion at position A; −540
   cc for a dry Muju; contributed +690 cc to White at position B purely from
   the two sides' waste difference (17 against 40).
5. **A banked crystal costs 110 cc below bank 8 and 45 cc above it while a
   crystal of material returns 100 cc.** `BankLiquid` and `BankConvertible`
   both read the same crystal. The kink at 8 and the −200 step at 6 mean the
   evaluator's preference between cash and bodies reverses twice across the
   bank range SU §1.6 rules "uncalibrated".
6. **`SpawnZero` and `Inv1SpawnZero` are the identical predicate scored
   twice**, −1600 cc for `area == 0 ∧ bank ≥ 3` (`geometry.ts:102` and
   `invariants.ts:290`). Rare but decisive when it fires; measured at both −800
   terms simultaneously in position A's unbuy perturbation.
7. **`RelocationDebt` charges 36 cc per action, not the declared
   `ACTION_VALUE_CC = 60`**, undiscounted, for relocations that are only
   projected.
8. **`Inv14LiquidityFloor` tests a state, not a spend.** SU §7.14 forbids
   spending the last 6–8 liquid crystals; `invariants.ts:343` penalises holding
   fewer than 6, whether or not anything was spent, waived by an unrelated kill.
9. **`RunwayCliff` and `Insolvency` fire on the same event at turn 1.** A side
   with `bank + income₁ < upkeep₁` takes −600 from `RunwayCliff` and
   −750 from `Insolvency` (`max(0, 6 − 1) = 5` at w = −150): −1350 cc for one
   condition.
10. **`turnsToInsolvency` projects a bill the rules will not permit.**
    `tables/economy.ts:175` holds the standing upkeep constant for six turns,
    but `src/game/upkeep.ts:18-31` forces a keep-set choice the moment the bank
    cannot pay, releasing tier-2+ units and lowering the bill. The projection's
    insolvency is counterfactual.

### Checked and found correct

- `EconResult.income[0]` equals `projectedIncome(p, side)` exactly: the first
  DP turn takes `min(mine, reserve)` off the unmodified reserve copy before any
  relocation can be triggered (`tables/economy.ts:199-207`). DESIGN §5.12.1 row
  25's `upkeepDue` and the DP's `upkeep[0]` likewise agree
  (`tables/economy.ts:175`, `core/income.ts:94-102`). So `RunwayCliff`'s
  implementation matches its definition.
- `PST_MINE` carries no rent term, as `core/income.ts:16-19` claims, and
  reproduces SU §1.2's discounted values exactly.
- `SpawnReserve` and `PstMine` do not overlap: `reserveSum` covers legal spawn
  squares only, and an occupied square is not legal (`core/spawn.ts:99`).
  Measured at position A, `SpawnReserve` is 0 while `PstMine` is 11.
- `economyStayInPlace` is exercised by `lab/hard-ai/oracles/economy.ts` against
  the canonical `endOfTurnIncome`/`upkeepDue` primitives; nothing in this audit
  contradicts that oracle. The findings above are about what the FEATURES do
  with the DP's output, not about the DP reproducing a stay-in-place
  simulation — with the single exception of finding 3, which is about the
  relocation branch the oracle only checks for monotonicity against
  stay-in-place, not against the reserve.

## Caveats

- Two positions. Position A is a constructed single-unit fixture and Position B
  is one of sixteen loss roots; neither is a sample.
- The two sweeps are constructed variants of Position A (bank set to 0..14; the
  `plant_1` replaced by a `fire_1` and the reserve swept). The fixture on disk
  is unchanged; the variants exist only inside the instrument's process.
- Position A has no Black units, so its symmetric differences read as White's
  absolute economy. That is what makes the per-crystal prices legible and also
  what makes its absolute score meaningless as a position assessment.
- Every misjudgment claim here rests on judge 3 (authored: SU and DESIGN) or
  judge 4 (canonical fact). None is supported by a game outcome or a foreign
  engine, and none has been priced by a contest row. A weight that contradicts
  SU may still be the weight that wins games.
- The `div100` truncation (`features.ts:144`) puts the measured per-crystal
  steps on a 100 cc grid per feature, so any single step can be off the derived
  value by up to 100 cc in either direction; only the sums are comparable.
- `boundStage2` (`features.ts:512-575`) means stage 2 can be skipped at a leaf.
  Where it is skipped, `PstMine`'s +60 stands alone and the sign inversion of
  finding 2 does not occur. Scores from the two paths are therefore not
  comparable crystal by crystal; the ledger above is the `full()` path, which
  is what the gates and the tuner use.
