Where the value sits

Mining M can eventually extract the top M layers of each well. These totals are inventories across the whole board, not income per turn. “Rich” components connect orthogonally through wells of at least four layers.

Describe each map

Rule count and neighboring depth changes are transparent descriptions of pattern complexity, not calibrated difficulty scores. E is the most elaborated candidate, not a claim that every strategic desideratum has been proven.

Unit roles, conditional tier access and search sensitivity

A role witness means the cheapest single piece meeting a stated geographic task. Counts depend on the task grid and are not strength rankings. Tier access uses one bank route’s income schedule, with no income feedback from upgrades, no protection costs and no extra income after turn 5. It is a conditional financing calculation, not optimal actual access.

Methods and limits

    The study does not certify first-player fairness, resistance to raids, or competitive opening diversity. Those require adversarial games with resource-aware opponents. The shipped game, board size and unit catalogue are untouched.
