# Exploratory follow-up

Written after inspecting main and first route batches. The D5 edge-anchor policy did not improve on the alternate. Geometric inspection shows why simply reaching a rich square is an incomplete adaptation: D5 is the only central 8 inside its own rectangle, and it is occupied by the anchor. It opens no empty central 8 for next-turn purchases. E5 opens D5; F5 opens D5, E5 and F4. Both cost four Hi movement actions from the opening position, versus three for D5. These are only geometrical opportunities: an enemy in the rectangle can block them before placement.

Run the same fixed policy with E5 and F5 first-turn anchors, preserving all remaining weights and decisions, against the same three opponents in both colors and maps at 20 seeds. This is a post-result exploratory test, not part of the original confirmatory screen. Do not select a winner and report it as pre-specified. Other policies and completed data are unchanged.
