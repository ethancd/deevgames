# E4.1 — cost and completion profile

E4 lane 1, 2026-09-18. `docs/hard-ai/e4/E4-PLAN.md` lane table row "E4.1 cost
and completion profile"; usefulness rule item 2 (attribution) and item 1
(completion). Instrument: `lab/hard-ai/bench/profile.ts` (`hard:profile`),
built without touching `src/ai/hard` — an in-process CPU profile (self time by
source file, ancestor-resolved for shared infrastructure) plus the engine's
own `RootResult.stats`/`.candidates`/`.rootTrace`/`.ply1` instrument
(`opts.expose`, `opts.ply1Trace`). Method and the one gap this instrument
could not close: `docs/hard-ai/e4/amendments/lane1.md`. Every number below
carries its artifact (`lab/results/hard-ai-e4/profile/<set>-<work>.json`) and
was produced by a single `hard@desktop` engine process, one search per
position, `git 437ee40f`, on this desktop (Apple M2 Max x12, node v24.11.1).
Champion identity check: `p8-wall3000.json`'s `resolvedConfigHash` is
`4e7afdf76b32fadfab2d11577cb610c8f9f1a491b5153e70b87b0774403600cd`, unchanged
(this lane owns no file under `src/ai/hard`).

## Sets

- **(a) p8** — 11 seat turns over 20 s from the six E3 correctness rows
  (`lab/results/hard-ai-e3/p8/slow-turns.json`), reconstructed via
  `analyze/replay.ts` from the rows' own replays
  (`lab/results/hard-ai-e3/ablate/{eval-fix-b3,eval-fix-b6,eval-correct-v1}/fixed100k/replays/`).
  These are the "difficult and depleted" positions the usefulness rule names.
- **(b) e1-losses** — the 12 distinct E1.1 loss positions
  (`lab/results/hard-ai-e3/loss-judgment/*.json`, `fileIds` resolved against
  `lab/results/hard-ai-e1/e1.1-diag/replays/`), the position the adviser
  search ran from in each case.
- **(c) e1-dev** — 16 of the 48 `e1-dev` openings
  (`lab/hard-ai/ladder/openings/e1-dev.jsonl`, first 16 by file order), each
  advanced from the opening to game turn 6 by a Rush-vs-Rush game
  (`lab/hard-ai/bench/profile.ts buildE1DevPositions`, seeds
  `20264100..20264115`, descriptive only — no ledger seed rule applies to
  development rows), white's turn at turn 6 taken as the position.

Each set was run at fixed:100,000, fixed:400,000 and wall:3000, nine
invocations, every one under the 5-minute cap (worst: p8 at fixed:100,000,
3 m 26 s wall; every other invocation under 2 minutes). **p8 at fixed:400,000
was not attempted**: none of the 11 positions has ever been measured past
fixed:100,000, the four positions safe enough to run there already spend most
of a 5-minute batch, and a fixed-work search reads no clock and cannot be
preempted once started — see `buildP8Positions`'s own skip reasons in
`lab/results/hard-ai-e4/profile/p8-fixed400000.json` (11/11 skipped, 0
attempted, 0.43 s to produce). At fixed:100,000, p8 itself keeps only the 4 of
11 positions whose OWN prior recorded cost at that exact rung
(`slow-turns.json`) leaves headroom; the other 7 are reached only at
wall:3000, which self-aborts (P8-SLOW-TURNS.md §3).

## The dominant cost per class of position

**Ordinary positions (sets b and e1-losses, c and e1-dev) are dominated by
TABLE CONSTRUCTION, not by search, evaluation or the prover.** Mean share of
profiled wall time, `tables` bucket:

| set | work | tables | prover | generation | evaluation | canonicalVerify | replySearch | other | sample |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| e1-losses | fixed:100,000 | 71.4% | 13.2% | 7.1% | 3.0% | 3.6% | 0.6% | 1.0% | n=12, `e1-losses-fixed100000.json` |
| e1-losses | fixed:400,000 | 72.7% | 12.6% | 6.6% | 2.8% | 3.3% | 0.6% | 1.4% | n=12, `e1-losses-fixed400000.json` |
| e1-losses | wall:3000 | 72.3% | 13.3% | 7.0% | 3.3% | 2.1% | 0.7% | 1.2% | n=12, `e1-losses-wall3000.json` |
| e1-dev | fixed:100,000 | 75.6% | 12.0% | 6.6% | 3.3% | 1.3% | 0.4% | 0.7% | n=16, `e1-dev-fixed100000.json` |
| e1-dev | fixed:400,000 | 75.5% | 11.9% | 6.2% | 3.2% | 2.0% | 0.4% | 0.8% | n=16, `e1-dev-fixed400000.json` |
| e1-dev | wall:3000 | 75.6% | 12.0% | 6.5% | 3.4% | 1.3% | 0.4% | 0.9% | n=16, `e1-dev-wall3000.json` |

Tables holds this 71-76% share regardless of work budget or wall vs fixed
mode — six independent samples agree to within 5 points. The two functions
behind it in every one of the six runs: `bfsFrom`/`bfsMulti`
(`src/ai/hard/core/movement.ts`, reachability BFS, attributed here to
`tables` because every sampled call's nearest classifiable ancestor is a
`tables/*.ts` frame — see `docs/hard-ai/e4/amendments/lane1.md`'s sibling
note in `profile.ts`'s `isSharedInfrastructure`) and `spawnGeometry`
(`src/ai/hard/tables/geometry.ts`). A third, `runKillDp`
(`src/ai/hard/tables/kill.ts`) or `strikeArea` (`src/ai/hard/tables/threat.ts`),
rounds out the top 3 depending on the sample (below).

**Pathological positions (set a, p8) invert this: PROVER dominates, and at
fixed:100,000 it is nearly the whole turn.**

| set | work | prover | tables | canonicalVerify | generation | evaluation | replySearch | sample |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| p8 | wall:3000 | 69.8% | 13.7% | 12.3% | 2.0% | 1.1% | 0.2% | n=11, `p8-wall3000.json` |
| p8 | fixed:100,000 | 97.0% | 2.3% | 0.1% | 0.3% | 0.1% | 0.0% | n=4 (7 skipped), `p8-fixed100000.json` |

At fixed:100,000 the top function by summed self time across the 4 positions
is `damageBoundCore` (`src/ai/hard/tactics/prover.ts`), 122,773.9 ms of
189,598.0 ms total profiled time (65%) by itself; `act` (same file) is
second, 54,899.1 ms (29%); together the two `prover.ts` functions are 93.7%
of the whole profiled window across 4 positions. This is P8's own hot leaf
(`docs/hard-ai/e3/P8-SLOW-TURNS.md` §1: "The hot leaf is `damageBoundCore`
(55,074 ms)") reproduced independently by a different instrument on 3
additional positions beyond the one P8 profiled by hand.

**The meter's own accounting under-prices this by two orders of magnitude.**
On `p8:correct-v1-desktop-black-seatTurn23` (fixed:100,000, the position
`docs/hard-ai/e3/P8-SLOW-TURNS.md` §2 reproduced): `byClass.PROVER = 32`
calls, priced at `WORK_COST[PROVER] = 40` units/call = 1,280 of the 100,711
units spent (1.3% of the rung) — while the profiler attributes 87,785 ms of
this turn's 87,785 ms `searchMs` (essentially all of it) to work whose leaf
functions are `damageBoundCore`/`act`/`reachableFrom`. This is P6's own
finding (`search/pvs.ts countProver`'s header: generation's prover calls are
COUNTED, never PRICED) confirmed from the wall-time side rather than the
meter side: `byClass` says the prover cost almost nothing; the CPU profile
says it was nearly the whole turn.

## Completed iterations and coverage distributions

**Completed iterations** (`stats.depth`, the count of iterations
`iterativeDeepening` finished — DESIGN §5.11.2):

| set | work | mean completed iterations | distribution | sample |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| e1-losses | fixed:100,000 | 2.00 | 12/12 positions at depth 2 | `e1-losses-fixed100000.json` |
| e1-losses | fixed:400,000 | 2.92 | 11/12 at depth 3, 1/12 at depth 2 (`g5-s11_3_11-B-white`) | `e1-losses-fixed400000.json` |
| e1-losses | wall:3000 | 2.17 | 9/12 at depth 2, 2/12 at depth 3, abort share 25.0% (3/12) | `e1-losses-wall3000.json` |
| e1-dev | fixed:100,000 | 2.00 | 16/16 positions at depth 2 | `e1-dev-fixed100000.json` |
| e1-dev | fixed:400,000 | 3.00 | 16/16 positions at depth 3 | `e1-dev-fixed400000.json` |
| e1-dev | wall:3000 | 2.00 | 16/16 at depth 2, abort share 6.2% (1/16) | `e1-dev-wall3000.json` |
| p8 | wall:3000 | 0.82 | depths {0,0,0,0,0,1,1,1,2,2,2}, abort share 81.8% (9/11) | `p8-wall3000.json` |
| p8 | fixed:100,000 | 2.25 | depths {2,2,3,2} on the 4 positions it could reach | `p8-fixed100000.json` |

Depth doubles from 100,000 to 400,000 units on ordinary positions
(2 -> 3 completed iterations, both sets, every position but one), matching
E2's "iteration cost ratio median 9.05 per depth" finding (`E4-PLAN.md`
"Why E4 now": 200k reaches depth 2-3, 400k is needed for the next). On p8,
`wall:3000`'s mean completed-iteration count (0.82) is BELOW 1: 5 of 11
positions complete no iteration at all before the deadline — the turn's
answer there is the P6 salvage (`search/root.ts pickUnsearched`) or the
generator's own ranking, never a searched value.

**Root coverage** (`RootResult.candidates`, `opts.expose`: candidates listed
vs searched, from the published iteration):

- Ordinary positions (b, c): 100% coverage — every listed root candidate on
  the published iteration was searched — across all 84 profiled position-runs
  in both sets (12+16 positions x3 work levels each), fixed:100,000,
  fixed:400,000 and wall:3000 alike, aborted wall-mode turns included (e.g.
  `e1-losses:g2-s20_3_15-A-white` at fixed:100,000: 28 listed, 28 searched;
  the same position at wall:3000 aborts and still shows 28/28). Root coverage
  is not where these positions spend their budget; iterative deepening simply
  never runs out of candidates to search at the depths it reaches.
- p8 (a), wall:3000: coverage collapses on the positions the deadline hits
  before or during the must-answer scan. Three of 11: `correct-v1-arm-white-
  seatTurn25` (20 listed, 0 searched), `b3-arm-black-seatTurn23` (30 listed, 0
  searched), `b3-arm-black-seatTurn22` (16 listed, 0 searched) — a full
  candidate list exists (the root generation completed) and NOTHING was
  applied to it before the abort. Two more are partial:
  `b3-desktop-white-seatTurn23` (28 listed, 7 searched),
  `correct-v1-desktop-black-seatTurn24` (31 listed, 4 searched). The
  remaining 6 of 11 reach full coverage (e.g. `b3-arm-black-seatTurn21`, 39/39;
  `correct-v1-arm-white-seatTurn24`, 43/43) — coverage on p8 is bimodal, not
  uniformly degraded.

**Depth-1 reply coverage** (`RootResult.ply1`, `opts.ply1Trace`; see
`docs/hard-ai/e4/amendments/lane1.md` for what this can and cannot say —
`meanGenerated`/`cutShare` are the closest available proxy for "replies
searched vs generated", not that count itself, because no existing hook
reports how many of a ply-1 node's generated replies `pvs`'s own loop applied
before a cutoff or truncation):

- Ordinary positions: every ply-1 node's generation ran to completion
  (`cutShare = 0` in all 84 profiled position-runs, both sets, every work level) —
  the generator's own stop-aware sink never truncated a ply-1 generation.
  Mean replies generated per ply-1 node against the interior generator's
  configured width (`engine.config.genInterior.K = 16`): e1-losses
  fixed:100,000 mean 20.2 per node (n=12 positions, ply1 node counts equal to
  `rootCoverage.searched` in every case, i.e. one ply-1 generation per
  searched root candidate on the published iteration); e1-dev fixed:100,000
  mean 25.4 per node (n=16). The generated count regularly EXCEEDS the
  configured `K` of 16 because forced injections (DESIGN §5.6) add to the
  generator's own beam rather than replacing it.
- p8 (a): `cutShare = 0` on every one of the 11 positions at every work level
  measured — the ply-1 generations this instrument DID observe were never
  truncated by the stop-aware sink either. But the ply-1 NODE COUNT itself
  collapses with root coverage: `correct-v1-arm-white-seatTurn25` and the two
  zero-root-coverage `b3` positions above show `ply1.nodes = 0` (no ply-1
  generation was ever reached, because no root candidate was ever searched);
  `correct-v1-desktop-black-seatTurn24` shows 4 ply-1 nodes against 31 root
  candidates listed. Where p8 loses coverage, it loses it before depth 1 is
  ever reached, not inside depth-1 generation itself.

**Abort share** (share of profiled position-runs whose `stats.stopReason` was
`'abort'`, wall mode only — fixed-work mode reads no clock and is never
`'abort'`): e1-losses wall:3000 25.0% (3/12); e1-dev wall:3000 6.2% (1/16); p8
wall:3000 81.8% (9/11). p8's abort share is 3-13x the ordinary sets' at the
SAME wall allowance — the single clearest completion-side signal separating
the two position classes.

## The three most expensive phases, file:function

Summed self time across every position-run this lane profiled (99 total
across 9 invocations: e1-losses 12 positions x3 work levels = 36, e1-dev 16
positions x3 work levels = 48, p8 11 positions at wall:3000 + 4 of 11 at
fixed:100,000 + 0 at fixed:400,000 = 15):

1. **`damageBoundCore` @ `src/ai/hard/tactics/prover.ts`** — 122,773.9 ms
   summed self time in `p8-fixed100000.json` alone (4 positions), plus
   28,743.4 ms in `p8-wall3000.json` (11 positions); this single function is
   the largest cost this lane measured anywhere, entirely on the p8 set.
   Bucket: `prover`.
2. **`bfsFrom` / `bfsMulti` @ `src/ai/hard/core/movement.ts`** — the two
   largest contributors on every ordinary-position run: 4,020.8 ms
   (`bfsFrom`, e1-dev fixed:100,000, n=16), 16,997.7 ms (e1-dev
   fixed:400,000), 2,008.3 ms + 1,951.1 ms (`bfsFrom`+`bfsMulti`, e1-losses
   fixed:100,000, n=12). Bucket: `tables` (ancestor-resolved — the frame
   itself has no phase; see the amendment note above).
3. **`enoughPossibleDamage` / `(anonymous)` @ `src/game/homeCheckmate.ts`** —
   14,443.7 ms and 12,182.2 ms summed self time in `p8-wall3000.json` (11
   positions); this is the canonical home-defense search P7 names
   (`docs/hard-ai/e3/P8-SLOW-TURNS.md` §3: "two thirds of the wall-mode
   residue is the canonical side"), reproduced here across the whole p8 set
   rather than the two positions P8 profiled by hand. Bucket: `canonicalVerify`.

`spawnGeometry` (`src/ai/hard/tables/geometry.ts`, from 1,311.3 ms summed self
time on `e1-losses-fixed100000.json` to 9,133.1 ms on
`e1-dev-fixed400000.json`) and `runKillDp` (`src/ai/hard/tables/kill.ts`) are
consistently 3rd-4th on the ordinary sets, behind the two `movement.ts`
functions; `strikeArea`
(`src/ai/hard/tables/threat.ts`) took the 1st slot instead of `bfsFrom` on
exactly one run (`e1-losses-fixed400000.json`, 6,625.1 ms vs 6,591.4 ms —
within noise of each other).

## What this instrument could not attribute

- **Depth-1 replies SEARCHED vs generated** (as opposed to generated vs the
  generator's configured width): no existing hook reports it;
  `docs/hard-ai/e4/amendments/lane1.md` files the gap and the workaround
  (`cutShare`/`meanGenerated` proxies) used above.
- **`src/ai/hard/core/state.ts`'s self time is bundled entirely into
  `prover`.** `Replica.make`'s own bookkeeping (apply/undo an action) and its
  occasional `provesHomeCheckmate` call share one file; self time cannot be
  split further without a call-site change to `core/state.ts`, which this
  lane does not own. Where `core/state.ts` shows up materially (it did not
  make the top 3 on any run above), the number should be read as an upper
  bound on the prover's own share.
- **`(garbage collector)` and empty-URL frames** land in `other` (0.1-1.4%
  of profiled time on every run) rather than any of the seven buckets — this
  is genuinely unattributable to a phase and is reported as such, not folded
  into the nearest guess.
- **The exposure instrument's own cost** (`opts.expose`, `opts.ply1Trace`) is
  bucketed separately as `instrument` and measured at 0.0% (rounded) of
  profiled time on every run in this campaign — consistent with
  `search/probe.ts`'s own header claim that it is observation-only and cheap
  when the search it is watching is not itself pathologically large.
