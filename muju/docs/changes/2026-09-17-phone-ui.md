- Gameplay fits the dynamic phone viewport, with compact live clocks and separate Room details.
- Pending summons can be tapped to preview stats and attack reach, including during an opponent's turn. Occupied squares have a separate summon inspection button.
- Both armies can be inspected during an online opponent's turn and during incoming playback. Reach uses a full turn's action budget; your own units keep their correct label. Inspection follows moving pieces, switches to pending summons, never sends actions, and resets when your turn resumes.
- Incoming remote moves play every action in order at the instant replay speed (300 ms Fast, 1,000 ms Slow). Step mode uses Slow for automatic playback. Clock updates stay live, and gameplay waits for playback to finish.
- Post-game Phasing analysis keeps the board square and visible within the phone viewport. Full-width compact review controls prevent the footer from squeezing the board; explicit height also corrects WebKit's coordinate-padding sizing. Reloading, navigating, inspecting pending summons, and exploring a private variation preserve the saved online result.
- Opening an invitation URL puts **Join a game** first in the lobby, with the shared name field and prefilled invitation together. Regular lobby visits keep Active games first. [Phone preview](invitation-lobby.png).
