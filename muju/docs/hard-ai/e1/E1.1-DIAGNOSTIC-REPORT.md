Before A11 the pilot measured `hard@lab` at p95 3274–3725 ms and max 4584 ms
(1.53x) with 22 overruns in 170 turns; with the deadline the worst turn is
1.10x and the rate is under the 5% void threshold by a factor of 20.

The mean turn is 1893 ms against 2969 ms for `aiv2-hard`, and that gap is NOT
a neutral design property (corrected after the independent review). Read off
`games.jsonl`: the hard seat's FIRST turn is deadline-cut in 26 of 32 games
(mean 2899 ms, median 3006), turns 6-7 hit the deadline in 10-13 of 32, and
later turns average 1600-1800 ms. Cause: the cold profile `INITIAL_UNITS_PER_MS`
= 200 against ~100 units/ms measured on this box under campaign load, so the
400k rung cannot finish in 3000 ms; the first sample then replaces the profile
(A16) and the rung oscillates between 200k and 400k, leaving roughly a third
of the allowance unused on ordinary turns. This is EPIC-PLAN §3's "time
allocation" bottleneck candidate and E1.4's first hypothesis; it is not
patched before the baseline. The artifacts cannot count deadline-cut searches
directly (`abortedSearches` is added to the timing record from A16 on).

E1.1 ran `--legality as-shipped` (0 illegal actions), the baseline runs
`--legality strict` (A7); with zero illegal actions the two modes coincide.
