# Gate 1 runner — adopted amendment A1

A1 was adopted in `0a6f8db5` on the integration branch. The runner pins that
commit, preregistration path and SHA256 in `../ai/gate1-references.json`, verifies
the Git object, and copies the adopted document into each new result directory.
This branch's working copy of the Claude-owned preregistration predates A1; it
is unchanged. Historical T2b results and the proposal remain historical evidence.

Acceptance is evaluated separately for each opponent and h0/h3 cell:

- Expand, Balanced and aiv2-medium: hard score > 0.5 and pair-aware 95% Elo
  lower bound > 0. Rush strength is reported only.
- The original scripted-bot purchase/inactivity bands apply unchanged to Rush,
  Expand and Balanced. The bands do not apply to the medium comparison.
- In **every** game against **any** opponent with more than 10 completed player
  turns (not rounds), hard must make at least one purchase commitment. Each
  violating game ID is reported; this check is independent of cell averages.
- Correctness vetoes remain: zero illegal actions, invariant failures, anomalies
  and adapter errors; exact schedule and identity. Missing/non-integer purchase
  or completed-turn evidence invalidates the report. Adopted runner mechanics
  retain the per-cell adjudication cap of 1%.

Historical Elo margins are audit metadata, not thresholds or execution
prerequisites. A complete full row can report `passed` or `failed`; malformed
evidence reports `invalid`. A pilot always reports `pilot-ineligible` even if
its observed criteria pass. An interval containing zero does not pass.

Mechanics are unchanged: hard 6,000 / medium 3,000 fixed-work budget per own
turn, 64 seat-mirrored pairs per opponent/handicap cell (1,024 games), seed
20260957. The pilot is one pair/cell (16 games), seed 20260956. Both begin at
the canonical initial Phasing position, use no opening corpus, and use the
machine-wide heavy queue. Evidence directories must be new.
